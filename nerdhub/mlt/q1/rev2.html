🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

PCA kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]: data mein $d$ features hain par asli structure kam dimensions mein hota hai. PCA woh orthonormal directions dhundhta hai jin par data ka variance sabse zyada hai — unhi par project karke data ko kam numbers mein likhte hain (compression), aur sabse kam error ke saath wapas bana sakte hain (reconstruction). Unsupervised learning ka pehla tool: koi label nahi chahiye.

Top 5 traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. Centring skip — uncentred $S$ ya $\tfrac1n\sum\|x_i\|^2$ (2.1 P7: $43.75$ vs $18.44$; P8 option 1, P9).
  2. $\lambda_1$ vs $\operatorname{tr}C$ vs $C_{jj}$, aur $\lambda_K$ ko variance likhna ($n$ se divide bhoolna) / $n$ ka numerator-only cancel.
  3. Normalise na karna — raw dot ($3$ vs $3/\sqrt5$), coefficient $u^Tx/u^Tu$ ko coordinate banana; PC candidates mein length check; dual mein non-unit $\beta$.
  4. $\tfrac1n$ vs $\tfrac1{n-1}$ (2.1 P2: $833$ vs $625$) aur threshold boundary ($94.2\%\neq95\%$, "$\ge$").
  5. Storage / rank: $kn$ ya $kd$ akela; rank ko $d$ ya $n$ samajhna; "same eigenvalues" ⇒ "same eigenvectors"; $\pm$ pair mein ek hi tick.
