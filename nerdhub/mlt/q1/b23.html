W1 · B22 (5.1)

Bernoulli data: $n=40$ points, $14$ ones. (a) MLE $\hat p$? (b) $\ell'(p)=0$ likh kar solve karo.

Show answer

(a) $\hat p=\dfrac{14}{40}=\mathbf{0.35}$. (b) $\ell(p)=14\ln p+26\ln(1-p)$, $\ell'(p)=\dfrac{14}p-\dfrac{26}{1-p}=0\Rightarrow14(1-p)=26p\Rightarrow p=\dfrac{14}{40}$. Aaj ka link: 5.2 mein bhi yahi 5 steps hain — sirf $f(x;\theta)$ badalta hai.

W2 · B22 (5.1 trap)

Ek hi coin: experiment 1 mein 10 tosses, 3 heads; experiment 2 mein 5 tosses, 2 heads. (a) Pooled MLE of $p$? (b) Dono estimates ka average kya deta hai?

Show answer

(a) Pooled: $\dfrac{3+2}{10+5}=\dfrac5{15}=\mathbf{\tfrac13}\approx0.333$. (b) Average: $\dfrac{0.3+0.4}2=0.35$ — galat (alag $n$ ka dhyan nahi rakha). Aaj ka link: i.i.d. data ka $\ell$ sum hota hai (L47), isliye counts jodte hain, estimates nahi.

🧭 Position — yeh kahan baithta hai

🧠 Intuition — plain language

Ab data 0/1 nahi hai — waiting times (Exponential), counts (Poisson), "heavy-tailed" sizes (Pareto). Har model ek family of curves $f(x;\theta)$ hai aur $\theta$ ek knob hai. Knob ki har position ek "kahani" hai ki data kaise bana. Likelihood $L(\theta)=\prod_if(x_i;\theta)$ bataata hai ki poora observed data us kahani ke andar kitna "expected" lagta hai. MLE woh knob position hai jis par data sabse kam surprising lagta hai.

Recipe wahi rehti hai; model badalne par sirf algebra badalti hai. Aur ek surprise: har model ka MLE ek "natural" function of data hai — Exponential mein $1/\bar x$, Poisson mein $\bar x$, Pareto mein $n/\sum\ln x_i$ — kyunki har model data ko ek alag "scale" (sum, sum of logs) par padhta hai.

Analogy (labelled): detective aur suspects [ADDED] — har $\theta$ ek suspect ki kahani hai. $L(\theta)$ = "yeh kahani poore saboot (saara data) ko kitna achhe se explain karti hai." MLE = woh kahani jisme saboot sabse kam coincidence lagte hain. Yeh kahan toot-ta hai: (i) detective suspects ko pehle se jaanti hai/maanti hai (prior); MLE koi prior nahi use karta — woh 5.3 (Bayes) mein aata hai; (ii) suspect ki "total probability 1" hoti hai, par $L(\theta)$ ka $\theta$ par area $1$ nahi hota; (iii) detective ke paas bahut kam suspects hote hain, yahan $\theta$ ek continuum hai (sirf P5 mein do hi).

Yeh kya NAHI hai:

  • $\hat\lambda=0.2857$ ka matlab "$\lambda=0.2857$ hone ki probability" nahi (S2, B22).
  • MLE hamesha "sample mean" nahi hota: Poisson mein $\bar x$ hai, par Exponential mein $1/\bar x$ aur Pareto mein $n/\sum\ln x_i$ (WE4).
  • Log-likelihood "likelihood of log data" nahi hai — $\ln$ function (likelihood) par lagta hai, data par nahi (Pareto mein $\ln x_i$ dikhta hai, par woh $\ln f$ ke andar se aata hai).
  • Likelihood mein density $f(x;\theta)$ ki value $1$ se bhi badi ho sakti hai (jaise $5x^4$ at $x=0.95$ $\approx4.07$) — yeh galti nahi, density probability nahi hai (D1).

📖 Formal version (Formula Protocol)

Block 1 — general recipe (L41)

$$L(\theta)=\prod_{i=1}^{n}f(x_i;\theta),\qquad \ell(\theta)=\ln L(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln f(x_i;\theta),\qquad \hat\theta=\arg\max_{\theta}\ell(\theta).$$

Symbols: $x_1,\dots,x_n$ = observed data; $f(x;\theta)$ = density (continuous data) ya PMF (discrete data), $\theta$ = anjaan parameter; $\ell$ = log-likelihood. Assumption: $x_i$ independent aur identically distributed (G2).

Words mein: har point ek factor $f(x_i;\theta)$ deta hai; saare factors multiply (independence). $\ln$ lene se product sum ban jaata hai, aur $\ln$ increasing hai, isliye $\arg\max L=\arg\max\ell$.

Notice that… jo bhi term $\theta$ par depend nahi karta (jaise Poisson ka $-\ln x_i!$) woh $\ell$ mein rehta hai, par $\hat\theta$ nikaalte waqt derivative mein ghayab ho jaata hai.

Block 2 — Exponential (L42)

$$f(x;\lambda)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\ (x\ge0)\ \Rightarrow\ \ell(\lambda)=n\ln\lambda-\lambda\sum_i x_i,\quad \ell'(\lambda)=\frac n\lambda-\sum_ix_i=0\ \Rightarrow\ \hat\lambda=\frac{n}{\sum_ix_i}=\frac1{\bar x}.$$

Symbols: $\lambda>0$ = rate (events per unit time); $\bar x=\frac1n\sum x_i$ = sample mean. Assumption: sab $x_i\ge0$, $\sum x_i>0$.

Words mein: Exponential ka mean $1/\lambda$ hota hai; data ka mean $\bar x$ us mean ka estimate hai, isliye rate ka estimate uska reciprocal.

Notice that… $\ell''(\lambda)=-n/\lambda^2<0$ har $\lambda>0$ par ⇒ concave ⇒ yeh stationary point unique global max (L48).

Block 3 — Poisson (L43, L45)

$$f(x;\lambda)=\frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^{x}}{x!}\ \Rightarrow\ \ell(\lambda)=\sum_i\big[-\lambda+x_i\ln\lambda-\ln(x_i!)\big]=-n\lambda+\Big(\sum_ix_i\Big)\ln\lambda-\sum_i\ln x_i!,\qquad \hat\lambda=\frac{\sum_ix_i}{n}=\bar x.$$

Symbols: $x_i\in\{0,1,2,\dots\}$ = counts; $\lambda>0$ = mean (aur variance) count. Assumption: counts i.i.d. Poisson($\lambda$). Frequency table (L45): agar value $v_j$ $c_j$ baar aaye to $n=\sum_jc_j$ aur $\sum_ix_i=\sum_jc_jv_j$.

Words mein: Poisson ka mean $\lambda$ hi hai, isliye sample mean seedha $\lambda$ ka estimate banta hai.

Notice that… Exponential mein $\bar x$ ka reciprocal aaya, Poisson mein $\bar x$ khud — kyunki mean ka role alag hai ($1/\lambda$ vs $\lambda$). $-\ln x_i!$ term $\lambda$ se free hai, par $\ell$ ke jawab (option) mein zaroor dikhta hai (P3).

Block 4 — Pareto (L44)

$$f(x;\theta)=\theta x^{-\theta-1}\ (x\ge1,\ \theta>1)\ \Rightarrow\ \ell(\theta)=n\ln\theta-(\theta+1)\sum_i\ln x_i,\quad \ell'(\theta)=\frac n\theta-\sum_i\ln x_i=0\ \Rightarrow\ \hat\theta=\frac{n}{\sum_i\ln x_i}.$$

Symbols: $\theta$ = tail index (bada $\theta$ ⇒ patli tail); $x_i\ge1$ (minimum value $x_m=1$). Assumption: $\sum\ln x_i>0$ aur result $\hat\theta>1$ hona chahiye (warna constraint toot-ta hai, D5).

Words mein: $\ln x_i$ data ka "scale-free" roop hai; $\theta$ ka estimate "average $\ln x$" ka reciprocal hai.

Notice that… $\ell''(\theta)=-n/\theta^2<0$; aur Pareto MLE bilkul Exponential MLE ki tarah hai, bas data $\ln x_i$ par (D5).

Block 5 — do-value parameter set (L46) — P5 ka case

$$\Theta=\{-1,\,1\},\quad L(-1)=\prod_i5x_i^4,\quad L(1)=\prod_i1=1\quad\Rightarrow\quad \hat\theta_{MLE}=\begin{cases}-1,&\prod_i5x_i^4\gt1\\ 1,&\prod_i5x_i^4\lt1\end{cases}$$

Symbols: $\theta$ sirf $-1$ ya $+1$ ho sakta hai; $-1$ ↔ density $5x^4$, $+1$ ↔ density $1$ ($0<x<1$). Assumption: sab $x_i\in(0,1)$ (warna dono likelihood $0$).

Words mein: do hi candidates hain — dono likelihood nikaalo aur bade ko chuno. Derivative ki koi zaroorat nahi (parameter continuous hai hi nahi).

Notice that… log mein yeh $\sum_i\ln(5x_i^4)\gtrless0$ banta hai, yaani har point ka ek "vote" (Visual 2).

Important insight: teeno distributions ke liye MLE recipe ek hi hai — sirf do chaar line ka algebra badalta hai. Aur $\ell''<0$ ka matlab "stationary point = answer" bina extra check ke (is range mein).

This is why P2, P4, P6 sirf "recipe ka plug-in" hain, P1/P3 "log-likelihood ka shape" pehchaanne ke hain, aur P5 ek alag sawaal hai jisme parameter set finite hai (calculus lagana hi nahi).

⚙️ How it works — numbered mechanism

  1. Model aur support padho: $f(x;\theta)$ kya hai, $x$ ka range aur $\theta$ ka range (jaise Pareto: $x\ge1$, $\theta>1$).
  2. Data ko summarise karo: $n$ ginti; sufficient quantity — Exponential mein $\sum x_i$, Poisson mein $\sum x_i$ (frequency table ho to $\sum c_jv_j$), Pareto mein $\sum\ln x_i$.
  3. Likelihood likho: $L=\prod f(x_i;\theta)$ — ya seedha $\ell=\sum\ln f$ (L41).
  4. $\ln f$ simplify karo aur sum andar le jao: Exponential $\ln\lambda-\lambda x$; Poisson $-\lambda+x\ln\lambda-\ln x!$; Pareto $\ln\theta-(\theta+1)\ln x$.
  5. Differentiate: $\ell'(\theta)$ nikaalo ($\theta$-free terms ghayab).
  6. Zero karo, solve karo: $\hat\theta$ = formula (L42–L44).
  7. Verify: $\ell''<0$; $\hat\theta$ support ke andar hai? (Pareto: $\hat\theta>1$); boundary / non-differentiable case to nahi? (WE3).
  8. Jawab ko sawaal ki bhasha mein padho: rate ya mean? decimals kitne? Intermediate logs kam se kam 3 decimal tak rakho (P6 ka trap).

Finite parameter set (P5): steps 3 ke baad seedha compare — $L(\theta_1)$ vs $L(\theta_2)$ (L46); steps 5–6 chhoot jaate hain.

Exam fast path: Exponential ⇒ $n/\sum x$; Poisson ⇒ $\sum c_jv_j/\sum c_j$; Pareto ⇒ $n/\sum\ln x$; "log-likelihood" MCQ ⇒ sirf sum-of-$\ln f$ wala option.

WE1 · simple — Exponential, 3 points [ADDED]

Data $\{1,2,3\}$, $f=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$. MLE?

WE2 · exam-level — Poisson, frequency table [ADDED]

Value $0,1,2,3$ ke counts $4,6,7,3$. $\hat\lambda$ aur $\ell''(\hat\lambda)$?

Step 1 (L45): $n=4+6+7+3=20$; $\sum x_i=0\cdot4+1\cdot6+2\cdot7+3\cdot3=0+6+14+9=29$. Step 2: $\ell(\lambda)=-20\lambda+29\ln\lambda-\text{const}$. Step 3: $\ell'(\lambda)=-20+\dfrac{29}\lambda=0\Rightarrow\hat\lambda=\dfrac{29}{20}=\mathbf{1.45}$. Step 4: $\ell''(\hat\lambda)=-\dfrac{29}{1.45^2}=\mathbf{-13.79}$ (code ✓) $<0$.

WE3 · tricky / counterexample — jahan derivative recipe fail ho jaati hai [ADDED]

Uniform$(0,\theta)$: $f(x;\theta)=\dfrac1\theta$ for $0\le x\le\theta$, warna $0$. Data $\{1.2,\ 0.4,\ 2.0,\ 0.9\}$. $\hat\theta$?

Step 1: Agar $\theta\lt\max x_i=2.0$ to koi $x_i$ support ke bahar ⇒ $L(\theta)=0$. Step 2: $\theta\ge2.0$ par $L(\theta)=\theta^{-4}$, jo ghatta hai. Step 3: $\ell'(\theta)=-\dfrac4\theta\neq0$ kahin bhi — derivative-zero recipe ka koi jawab nahi. Maximum support ke kinare $\theta=2.0$ par hai ⇒ $\hat\theta=\max x_i=\mathbf{2.0}$.

Check (code ✓): $L(1.9)=0$, $L(2.0)=0.0625$, $L(2.5)=0.0256$, $L(3.0)=0.0123$. Seekh: "$\ell'=0$ solve karo" hamesha kaam nahi karta; pehle $L$ ka shape dekho (monotone? support-dependent?). Yeh syllabus ke bahar ka limit-case hai, sirf recipe ki hadd dikhane ke liye; bank ke 5.2 mein nahi aaya.

WE4 · non-example — "MLE = sample mean" hamesha nahi [ADDED]

🔬 Deeper insight (D3 — collapsible)

D1 · Density ≠ probability — $f(x)$ $1$ se bada ho sakta hai

Continuous data mein $f(x;\theta)$ density hai: $P(x\le X\le x+\varepsilon)\approx f(x;\theta)\,\varepsilon$ chhote $\varepsilon$ ke liye. $P5$ mein $f(0.95\mid\theta=-1)=5\cdot0.95^4=4.07>1$ — bilkul theek, kyunki $[0,1]$ par $\int5x^4dx=1$. Likelihood ke liye hamen sirf ratio chahiye ($L(\theta_1)/L(\theta_2)$), jisme $\varepsilon^n$ cancel ho jaata hai — isliye density ki value seedha product mein daalna jaayaz hai.

D2 · Concavity ek-ek model ke liye (L48)

Exponential: $\ell''=-\dfrac n{\lambda^2}$. Poisson: $\ell'=-n+\dfrac S\lambda$, $\ell''=-\dfrac S{\lambda^2}$ ($S=\sum x_i>0$). Pareto: $\ell''=-\dfrac n{\theta^2}$. Teeno har valid parameter par negative ⇒ $\ell$ strictly concave ⇒ jo bhi $\ell'=0$ ka solution hai woh unique global maximum hai (zaroorat nahi ki alag se boundary check karo, jab tak solution allowed range ke andar ho). Numbers: P2 par $-73.5$, P4 par $-9.92$, P6 par $-3.79$ (code ✓).

D3 · Teeno ek hi shape — $\ell(\theta)-\ell(\hat\theta)=c\,(\ln r-r+1)$ [ADDED]

$r=\theta/\hat\theta$. Exponential: $\ell(\lambda)-\ell(\hat\lambda)=n\ln r-S(\lambda-\hat\lambda)$ aur $\hat\lambda S=n$ ⇒ $=n(\ln r-r+1)$. Poisson: $=S\ln r-n(\lambda-\hat\lambda)$ aur $n\hat\lambda=S$ ⇒ $=S(\ln r-r+1)$. Pareto: $=n\ln r-S_{\ln}(\theta-\hat\theta)$ aur $\hat\theta S_{\ln}=n$ ⇒ $=n(\ln r-r+1)$. Isliye $c=n$ (Exp, Pareto) ya $c=\sum x_i$ (Poisson), aur $\ell''(\hat\theta)=-c/\hat\theta^2$. (Identity teeno datasets ke liye $r=0.5,\ 2$ par code-verified.) Matlab: "kitna data" ek hi number $c$ mein simat jaata hai — jitna bada $c$, utna patla peak (Visual 3).

D4 · Rate vs mean — invariance (L40) ka ek aur use [ADDED]

Exponential ko mean $\mu=1/\lambda$ se parametrise karo: $f=\dfrac1\mu e^{-x/\mu}$. Tab $\ell(\mu)=-n\ln\mu-\dfrac{\sum x_i}\mu$, $\ell'=-\dfrac n\mu+\dfrac{\sum x_i}{\mu^2}=0\Rightarrow\hat\mu=\bar x$. Invariance se $\hat\lambda=1/\hat\mu=1/\bar x$ — wahi jo seedha nikla. Yaani "sample mean" mean parameter ka MLE hai; rate ka MLE uska reciprocal.

D5 · Pareto ↔ Exponential, aur constraint $\theta>1$ [ADDED]

Agar $X$ Pareto($\theta$) hai ($x_m=1$) to $Y=\ln X$ ke liye $P(Y>t)=P(X>e^t)=\int_{e^t}^\infty\theta x^{-\theta-1}dx=e^{-\theta t}$, yaani $Y\sim\text{Exponential}(\theta)$. Isliye Pareto MLE = Exponential MLE $\ln$-data par: $\hat\theta=1/\bar y$ (P6: $1/0.870725=1.14847$ ✓ code). Constraint: P6 mein $\theta>1$ chahiye aur $\hat\theta=1.1485$ sirf $0.148$ upar hai. Agar $\sum\ln x_i\ge n$ ho to $\hat\theta\le1$ aata hai; $\ell'=\dfrac n\theta-S_{\ln}<0$ saare $\theta>1$ par ⇒ $\ell$ ghat-ta hi ghatta hai, supremum $\theta\to1^+$ par jo allowed nahi — "MLE exist nahi karta" (jaise data $\{e^2,e^2,e^2\}$: $\hat\theta=3/6=0.5<1$).

D6 · P5 ko geometric mean ke roop mein padho; tie aur support [ADDED]

$\hat\theta=-1\iff\prod5x_i^4>1\iff n\ln5+4\sum\ln x_i>0\iff\Big(\prod x_i\Big)^{1/n}>5^{-1/4}\approx0.669$. Yaani geometric mean $0.669$ se bada ho to $\theta=-1$, chhota ho to $\theta=+1$. Example sample $(0.95,0.9,0.4,0.5)$: GM $=0.643<0.669$ ⇒ $\hat\theta=+1$ (Visual 2 ke saath consistent ✓ code). Yeh "log-ratio vote" M1 ko dobara padhna hai (alag method nahi). M1 · Seedha derive (General)

Step 2: Exponential MLE (P2): $\hat\theta=\dfrac1{\bar y}$, $\bar y=\dfrac15\sum\ln x_i$.
