Top traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. $X^TX$ vs $XX^T$ — $X$ $d\times n$ hai to normal equations $XX^Tw=Xy$ ($d\times d$).
  2. Sum vs mean — MSE $=L/n$; gradient mein $\tfrac2n$ ya $2$ (sum loss) ka fark; $L$ mein $\tfrac2n$ bhoolna.
  3. "Convex ⇒ koi bhi $\eta$" aur $\eta=2/L$ ko convergent maanna — $\eta\ge2/L$ par error factor $|1-\eta L|\ge1$.
  4. SGD "guaranteed" — constant $\eta$ par noise floor (exact-fit data mein hi exact converge).
  5. Training MSE $0$ ≠ achhi generalisation; stem ke "$y=wx$" ko scalar padhna (asal mein $w^Tx$).

Show all answers (timer khatam hone ke baad)

RT1: $w=1.929$, MSE $=0.643$. $\hat w=\dfrac{\sum xy}{\sum x^2}=\dfrac{2+10+15}{1+4+9}=\dfrac{27}{14}=1.929$. Residuals: $-0.071,\ -1.143,\ 0.786$; squares jod $=1.929$; MSE $=\dfrac{1.929}3=0.643$. Trap: sum $1.929$ ko MSE likhna.

RT2: gradient $-18$; $w_1=0.9$. $\nabla\text{MSE}(0)=\dfrac2n\sum x(wx-y)=\dfrac23(1\cdot(-2)+2\cdot(-5)+3\cdot(-5))=\dfrac23(-27)=-18$. $w_1=0-0.05\cdot(-18)=0.9$. Trap: $\tfrac2n$ chhodna ($-27$ ⇒ $1.35$).

RT3: (a), (b), (c). $L=\dfrac2n\sum x^2=\dfrac23\cdot14=9.333$; $2/L=0.2143$, $1/L=0.107$. $\eta\lt0.2143$ ⇒ (a) $0.05$, (b) $0.10$ (monotone, $\lt1/L$), (c) $0.20$ (zigzag, factor $1-0.2\cdot9.333=-0.867$) converge. (d) $0.22$ ⇒ factor $-1.053$ diverge ✗; (e) ✗. Code: simulation mein $0.05,0.10,0.20$ ne $1.9286$ par tike; $0.22$, $0.30$ ne blow-up kiya ✓.

RT4: (a), (c), (e). (a) ✓. (b) ✗ $X$ $d\times n$ ho to $XX^Tw=Xy$ ($X^TX$ $n\times n$ hota). (c) ✓ ($L=n\cdot\text{MSE}$, constant factor). (d) ✗ best fit ka MSE tabhi $0$ jab data exactly fit ho. (e) ✓ convex ⇒ local $=$ global.

RT5: $w=(2,-1)$, MSE $=0$. $XX^T=\begin{bmatrix}14&15\\15&21\end{bmatrix}$, $Xy=(13,\,9)$ ⇒ $w=(2,-1)$. Check: $2-1=1$ ✓, $6-2=4$ ✓, $4-4=0$ ✓ — exact fit ⇒ MSE $0$ (code ✓). Trap: $X^TX$ ($3\times3$, singular) use karna.

RT6: (c), (d). (a) ✗ convex loss mein bura local minimum hota hi nahi. (b) ✗ koi shart nahi di — noise floor. (c) ✓ exact fit mein har point ka gradient $w^*$ par $0$ (B26: $w=(1,-2)$ par converge hua). (d) ✓ error factor $|1-\eta L|\gt1$. (e) ✗ $\eta=2/L$ par $\pm$ oscillation (code: $w_t=\pm w_0$).
