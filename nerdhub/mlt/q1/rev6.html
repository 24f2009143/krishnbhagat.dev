🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

T6 kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]: GMM ke log-likelihood mein $\ln$ ke andar sum hai, isliye MLE ka seedha formula nahi milta. Jensen kehta hai ki concave $\ln$ ke liye "average ka $\ln$" "$\ln$ ka average" se bada hota hai — isse sum ke andar ek lower bound milta hai jise EM baar-baar upar uthata hai. Aur convexity hi batati hai ki regression (T8) mein koi bura local minimum nahi hota.

Trigger → first move

Trigger phraseFirst move
"for every convex $f$ … choices of $\lambda$"Do check: har $\lambda_i\ge0$? $\sum=1$? Dono haan ⇒ ✓
"$\ln$ of a sum" vs "sum of $\ln$"$\ln$ concave ⇒ $\ln\sum\lambda a\ge\sum\lambda\ln a$
"which inequality holds for $f=\dots$"$f''$ ka sign: $\ge0$ ⇒ $\le$ (convex), $\le0$ ⇒ $\ge$
Option mein negative weight / sum $\ne1$Counterexample: $f=x^2$ ($a=e_i$) ya $f\equiv\pm1$
"upper bound on $f(c)$, $f$ convex, $f(a)$, $f(b)$ diye"$c=\lambda a+(1-\lambda)b$ likho; bound $=\lambda f(a)+(1-\lambda)f(b)$ [ADDED]

Top traps (subtopic page se):

  1. Sirf sum $=1$ dekhna — option 1 aur 3 ka sum $1$ hai phir bhi ✗ (negative weight).
  2. Sirf equal weights ko valid maanna — $(0.5,0.25,0.25)$ bhi valid hai.
  3. $\ln$ par inequality ulti likhna — $\ln$ concave hai, isliye "average ka $\ln$" bada: $\ln2.5=0.916$ jabki $\tfrac12\ln2+\tfrac12\ln3=0.896$ (weights $\tfrac12,\tfrac12$ par) — pehla bada.

⏱️ 5. Quick revision test

Rules: 5 questions · 15 marks (3 each) · 10 minutes. T6 ke concept aur 2026 Jul Q7 ke pattern par naye numbers (remix) [ADDED]; answers hidden. Ek hi baithak, notes band, calculator allowed. Chat mein: RT1: … | RT2: … | Confidence per Q: S/U/G

  1. RT1 · MSQ · 3 marks. $f$ convex hai. Kaun se $(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)$ har convex $f$ aur har $a_1,a_2,a_3$ ke liye $f(\sum\lambda_ia_i)\le\sum\lambda_if(a_i)$ guarantee karte hain?

    (a) $(0.2,\ 0.3,\ 0.5)$   (b) $(0.6,\ 0.6,\ -0.2)$   (c) $(0.5,\ 0.5,\ 0.5)$   (d) $(1,\ 0,\ 0)$   (e) $(0.25,\ 0.25,\ 0.5)$

  2. RT2 · SA · 3 marks. $f(x)=x^2$, $a_1=1$, $a_2=5$, weights $\lambda=(0.25,\ 0.75)$. $\sum\lambda_if(a_i)-f(\sum\lambda_ia_i)$ kya hai?

  3. RT3 · SA · 3 marks. Weights $(0.5,\ 0.25,\ 0.25)$, $a=(1,\ 4,\ 16)$. $\ln\big(\sum\lambda_ia_i\big)-\sum\lambda_i\ln a_i$ (3 decimals)?

  4. RT4 · MSQ · 3 marks. Kaun se functions ke liye $f(\sum\lambda_ia_i)\le\sum\lambda_if(a_i)$ har valid weights aur har point ke liye sach hai (domain mein)?

    (a) $f(x)=e^x$ on $\mathbb R$   (b) $f(x)=\ln x$ on $x>0$   (c) $f(x)=|x|$ on $\mathbb R$   (d) $f(x)=x^3$ on $\mathbb R$   (e) $f(x)=3x+2$ on $\mathbb R$

  5. RT5 · SA · 3 marks. $f$ convex hai, $f(2)=3$ aur $f(6)=11$. $f(5)$ ka sabse achha upper bound jo sirf in do values se nikalta hai?

Show all answers (timer khatam hone ke baad)

RT1: (a), (d), (e). Rule: har $\lambda_i\ge0$ aur sum $1$. (a) $0.2+0.3+0.5=1$ ✓. (b) sum $1$ par $-0.2\lt0$ ✗ (counterexample: $f=x^2$, $a=(0,0,1)$: LHS $f(-0.2)=0.04$, RHS $-0.2$). (c) sum $1.5$ ✗. (d) $(1,0,0)$ valid — dono side $f(a_1)$ ✓. (e) sum $1$ ✓.

RT2: 3. $\sum\lambda f(a)=0.25\cdot1+0.75\cdot25=19$; $\sum\lambda a=0.25+3.75=4$, $f(4)=16$; gap $=19-16=3$. Variance check: $\lambda_1\lambda_2(a_1-a_2)^2=0.1875\times16=3$ ✓. Trap: $f$ ko average karne ke bajay seedha $(1+25)/2=13$ ya $f(3)=9$ (equal weights).

RT3: 0.665. $\sum\lambda a=0.5+1+4=5.5$, $\ln5.5=1.7047$. $\sum\lambda\ln a=0+0.25(1.3863)+0.25(2.7726)=1.0397$. Antar $=0.665\ge0$ ✓ (concave $\ln$ ⇒ pehla bada). Trap: dono side ulat dena.

RT4: (a), (c), (e). (a) $e^x$: $f''=e^x>0$ ✓. (b) $\ln x$ concave ⇒ ulta ✗. (c) $|x|$ convex (triangle inequality) ✓. (d) $x^3$: $f''=6x$ ka sign badalta hai ⇒ convex nahi ✗ (counterexample: $\lambda=(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$, $a=(-2,0)$: LHS $f(-1)=-1$, RHS $\tfrac12(-8)+\tfrac12(0)=-4$ ⇒ $-1\le-4$ galat). (e) linear ⇒ barabari ✓.

RT5: 9. $5=\lambda\cdot2+(1-\lambda)\cdot6\Rightarrow\lambda=\tfrac14$. Bound: $\tfrac14\cdot3+\tfrac34\cdot11=9$. Trap: weights ulat dene par $\tfrac34\cdot3+\tfrac14\cdot11=5$ aata hai, jo galat hai ($5=\tfrac14\cdot2+\tfrac34\cdot6$, isliye $f(2)$ ka weight $\tfrac14$).

Scoring: SA = full ya zero; MSQ = saare sahi options, koi galat nahi. 15 mein se 12+ = T6 revision-ready. Saare numerical answers python se code-verified. Galtiyan chat mein error-tag ke saath bhejo.
