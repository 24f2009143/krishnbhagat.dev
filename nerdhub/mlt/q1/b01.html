🌍 7. Big picture: MLT ki poori story [ADDED]

Is bank ke 440 marks mein se sirf 7 (Regression) supervised learning ke hain — baaki sab unsupervised learning hai: hamare paas data hai, labels nahi. Data ko ek matrix ki tarah rakhte hain:

$$X = \begin{bmatrix} | & | & & | \\ x_1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_n \\ | & | & & | \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times n}$$

Yahan $d$ = features ki sankhya (har point kitne numbers se bana hai), $n$ = points ki sankhya, aur $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$ = $i$-th data point (ek column). Course ke saare PYQs isi convention ($d\times n$, points as columns) ko use karte hain — yeh yaad rakhna, kyunki $XX^T$ ($d\times d$) aur $X^TX$ ($n\times n$) ka fark isi se aata hai.

Ab is data se hum teen sawaal poochte hain — aur har sawaal ek topic family ban jaata hai:

Q1. "Data ko kam numbers mein kaise likhein?" → Representation

PCA (T2): best lines/subspace dhundo jis par project karne se sabse kam information jaaye. Kernel PCA (T3): agar structure curved hai, pehle data ko higher-dimensional space mein "map" karo, phir PCA.

Q2. "Data mein groups kaunse hain?" → Clustering

K-means (T4): har point ko ek hi cluster (hard assignment). GMM + EM (T7): har point ko har cluster mein ek probability (soft assignment).

Q3. "Kaunsa probability model is data ko generate karta hai?" → Estimation

MLE (T5): woh parameter jo observed data ko sabse likely banaye. Bayesian (T5): parameter ke baare mein prior belief + data → posterior belief.

Tools jo sab jagah lagte hain

Linear Algebra (T1): projection, eigenvectors. Convexity (T6): Jensen's inequality — EM ka backbone. Regression (T8): supervised learning ki pehli jhalak — loss minimise karna.

Analogy [ADDED]: Socho tumhare paas ek bade school ke saare students ka data hai (height, weight, marks, …).

  • PCA = "har student ko 50 numbers ki jagah 2–3 summary scores se describe karo" (jaise ek 'overall size' score).
  • K-means / GMM = "students ko natural groups mein baanto" — K-means pakka bolta hai "tum group 2 ho", GMM bolta hai "70% group 2, 30% group 1".
  • MLE / Bayes = "pass hone ki probability $p$ kya hai?" — MLE sirf data dekhta hai; Bayes pichle saal ka anubhav (prior) bhi milata hai.

Analogy kahan toot-ti hai: real students mein features ke units alag hote hain (cm vs kg), aur PCA scale-sensitive hai — analogy yeh nahi batati. Aur "natural groups" ki definition K-means ke liye sirf distance to mean hai, insaani samajh nahi.

Yeh kya NAHI hai: MLT ka yeh part classification/neural networks nahi padhata; regression bhi sirf loss + gradient descent ke level par aata hai (bank mein 3 scorable questions).

🧭 8. Har topic kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]

Har card: problem → core idea → signature formula → bank mein kaisa dikhta hai.

T1 · Linear Algebra

Problem: ek vector ko ek direction ke "along" aur "perpendicular" hisson mein kaise todein?

Idea: projection = shadow on a line.

$$\text{proj}_w(x) = \frac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}\,w$$

Bank: projection length, reconstruction error (Oct 2024). Andar se PCA ke har coordinate question mein.

T2 · PCA

Problem: high-dimensional data ko kam dimensions mein, minimum information loss ke saath.

Idea: sabse zyada variance wali directions = covariance ke top eigenvectors.

$$C=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_ix_i^T,\quad Cw=\lambda w$$

Bank: variance along PC, PC coordinates, explained variance, storage, dual PCA.

T3 · Kernels

Problem: PCA sirf straight lines/flat subspaces pakadta hai; curved structure miss.

Idea: $\phi(x)$ se higher dimension mein le jao — par $\phi$ explicitly compute kiye bina, sirf dot products se.

$$k(x,y)=\phi(x)^T\phi(y)$$

Bank: find φ, valid kernel?, polynomial/Gaussian values, kernel PCA facts.

T4 · K-means

Problem: unlabelled points ko $K$ groups mein baantna.

Idea: alternate karo — nearest mean ko assign, phir mean update (Lloyd's algorithm).

$$F=\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\|^2$$

Bank: run to convergence, objective monotonic, K-means++ probabilities.

T5 · Probability / MLE / Bayes

Problem: data dekh kar model ka parameter estimate karna.

Idea: likelihood maximise (MLE) ya prior × likelihood → posterior (Bayes).

$$\hat\theta_{ML}=\arg\max_\theta\sum_i\log f(x_i;\theta)$$

Bank: Bernoulli/Poisson/exponential/Pareto MLE, Beta posterior mean/mode.

T6 · Convexity

Problem: log of a sum ko handle kaise karein (GMM likelihood)?

Idea: convex/concave function ke liye Jensen's inequality — weights non-negative aur sum = 1.

$$f\Big(\sum_j\lambda_ja_j\Big)\le\sum_j\lambda_jf(a_j)$$

Bank: 1 MSQ (Jul 2026) — par EM ka proof isi par khada hai.

T7 · GMM + EM

Problem: clusters overlapping hain, hard assignment galat lagta hai.

Idea: har cluster ek Gaussian; E-step = responsibilities, M-step = weighted MLE.

$$\lambda_k^i=\frac{\pi_k\,\mathcal N(x_i\mid\mu_k,\sigma_k^2)}{\sum_j\pi_j\,\mathcal N(x_i\mid\mu_j,\sigma_j^2)}$$

Bank: responsibilities, π/μ updates, likelihood form, EM vs Lloyd.

T8 · Regression

Problem: labels $y$ diye hain; input se predict karna.

Idea: squared error minimise karo (closed form ya gradient descent).

$$\min_w\ \frac1n\sum_i (w^Tx_i-y_i)^2$$

Bank: fit $w$, MSE, GD convergence (Jul 2025 only).

🕸️ 9. Relationship + dependency map [ADDED]

Dependency map in words (dependencies chain by chain)
  1. Projection → PCA → Kernel PCA: PCA = "best line par projection". Dual PCA mein covariance ($XX^T$, $d\times d$) ki jagah $X^TX$ ($n\times n$) use hota hai — aur yahi $X^TX$ kernel PCA mein Gram matrix $K$ ban jaata hai. Isliye 2.6 (dual PCA) ke baad hi T3.
  2. Distance + mean → K-means → GMM: K-means ka mean update = "squared distance ka minimiser". GMM wahi kaam soft weights se karta hai; EM vs Lloyd ka comparison is chain ka last link hai.
  3. Probability → MLE → GMM M-step: GMM ke M-step ke formulas sirf "weighted MLE" hain — agar Bernoulli/Gaussian MLE clear hai to M-step free mein mil jaata hai.
  4. Convexity → Jensen → EM: $\log$ concave hai, toh $\log\sum \ge \sum\log$ (weighted) — isse EM ka lower bound banta hai jo har iteration mein likelihood ko kam nahi hone deta.
  5. Calculus → Regression: MSE convex hai (bowl-shaped), isliye gradient descent global minimum tak jaata hai — Jul 2025 MSQ ka core.
