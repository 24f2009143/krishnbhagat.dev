🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

Kernels kyun exist karte hain [ADDED]: PCA sirf linear (seedhi) directions dhundhta hai. Agar structure curved hai, to pehle data ko $\phi(x)$ se higher-dimensional space mein bhejo, aur wahan linear method chalao. Magic yeh hai ki $\phi$ explicitly kabhi nahi banana padta — algorithms sirf dot products $\phi(x)^T\phi(y)=k(x,y)$ maangte hain, aur woh seedha formula se milta hai (kernel trick).

Top 5 traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. $\sqrt2$ / $\sqrt c$ bhoolna aur $\phi$ ko unique maan lena; constant bhi ek coordinate hai.
  2. Polynomial kernel mein $+1$ bhoolna; feature count mein $d+m-1$ ($20$) vs $d+m$ ($35$); Gaussian mein norm ka square / $2\sigma^2$.
  3. Symmetry swap na karna ($x_1^2+y_2^2$, $\sin x\cos y\,k$); minus sign ya negative coefficient bina diagonal dekhe valid maan lena.
  4. PSD ≠ entries $\ge0$ / diagonal positive; Cauchy–Schwarz ya $2\times2$ pass ≠ valid ($3\times3$ Gram fail ho sakti hai); raw distance $\|x-y\|$ valid nahi.
  5. Kernel PCA mein centring: $K$ ko $K^c$ maan lena, $+P$ bhoolna, $\lambda_{K^c}$ ko variance likhna ($\div n$), "components $\le d$".
