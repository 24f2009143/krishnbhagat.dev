Consider a dataset with 1000 samples, where each sample belongs to $\mathbb R^{20}$. PCA is run and the top 5 principal components are retained, while the rest are discarded. If it takes one unit of memory to store a real number, find the percentage decrease in storage space of the dataset by moving to its compressed representation. Enter the answer correct to two decimal places.

🧠 Intuition — plain language

  • Compression = dataset ko kam numbers mein likhna. Har point ko poore $d$ numbers ke badle sirf $k$ scores ($z_1,\dots,z_k$) mein likho, aur ek baar sab ke liye $k$ PC directions ($d\times k$) store karo. Wapas banane ke liye: $\hat x=\sum_{j\le k}z_jw_j$.
  • Residue = jo compression mein kho gaya. $r=\tilde x-\hat x$ wahi hissa hai jo discarded PCs ki directions mein tha: $r=\sum_{j>k}z_jw_j$. Yeh kept PCs ke perpendicular hota hai.
  • Average loss discarded eigenvalues ka jod hai. $\tfrac1n\sum\|r_i\|^2=\sum_{j>k}\lambda_j$ — B10 ka tail-budget yahi hai. Bada eigenvalue rakho to loss kam.
  • "Rounds" = ek ek line nikalte jao. Round 1: best line, projection subtract. Round 2: bache hue residues par best line. Jab residues zero ho jayein, kaam khatam; jitne round lage utna hi rank hai.
  • Storage ka sawaal pure counting hai. Raw: $dn$ numbers. Compressed: $kd$ (directions) $+\;kn$ (scores) $=k(d+n)$. Fayda tabhi jab $k(d+n)<dn$.
  • Data jab pehle se low-dimensional ho (4-dim subspace mein 30-dim points): sirf 4 eigenvalues nonzero, 4 rounds ke baad residue zero, aur 4 PCs se poora dataset reconstruct hota hai — lossless compression.

Analogy [ADDED] — passport photo ka thumbnail + recipe. Poori photo ($d$ pixels) store karne ki jagah ek chhota "recipe": kuch standard patterns ($k$ PC directions, sab photos ke liye ek baar) aur har photo ke liye kuch weights ($k$ scores). Wapas banane par photo thodi dhundli aati hai; dhundlapan (residue) wahi hai jo discarded patterns mein tha.

Analogy kahan tootti hai: (1) Photo thumbnail mein pixels ghatate hain; PCA mein coordinates ghatte hain, points ka count wahi rehta hai ($n$). (2) Standard patterns fixed library se nahi, data se seekhe jaate hain, isliye woh bhi store karne padte hain ($kd$). (3) Thumbnail ka dhundlapan har jagah barabar hota hai; PCA mein loss sirf kam-variance directions mein hota hai.

Yeh kya NAHI hai:

  • "Storage $=kn$" ya "$=kd$" — nahi. Dono chahiye: $k(d+n)$ (directions + scores).
  • "Reconstruction error = $x-\hat x$ ka alag-alag line ke errors ka jod" — nahi. Ek saath $k$ PCs par project karo (P2 ka option 2 yahin galat hai).
  • "Reconstruction error = captured variance" — nahi. Captured $\sum_{j\le k}\lambda_j$ hai, error $\sum_{j>k}\lambda_j$ hai; dono mil kar $V_{\text{tot}}$.
  • "$k$ PCs rakhne se hamesha storage kam hoga" — nahi. $k\ge\tfrac{dn}{d+n}$ par saving $0$ ya negative ho jaati hai.
  • "Reconstruction ka matlab original wapas mil gaya" — sirf $k\ge$ rank par (residue zero); warna approximation. Aur original coordinates mein $\mu$ wapas jodna padta hai.

📊 Visual intuition

📖 Formal version — Formula Protocol

$$\text{(L1)}\ \ \hat x=W_kW_k^T\tilde x=\sum_{j=1}^{k}z_j\,w_j,\qquad z_j=w_j^T\tilde x$$ $$\text{(L2)}\ \ r=\tilde x-\hat x=\sum_{j>k}z_j\,w_j,\qquad r\perp w_1,\dots,w_k$$ $$\text{(L3)}\ \ \|r\|^2=\|\tilde x\|^2-\sum_{j\le k}z_j^2=\sum_{j>k}z_j^2$$ $$\text{(L4)}\ \ E_k=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|r_i\|^2=\sum_{j>k}\lambda_j=\bigl(1-\text{CEV}(k)\bigr)V_{\text{tot}}$$ $$\text{(L5)}\ \ E_k=0\iff k\ge\operatorname{rank}(C)\le\min(d,\,n-1)$$ $$\text{(L6)}\ \ \text{stored}=k(d+n)\ \ \text{vs raw}\ dn,\qquad \text{decrease}\,\%=100\Bigl(1-\frac{k(d+n)}{dn}\Bigr)$$ $$\text{(L7)}\ \ E(w)=\frac1n\sum_{i}\|\tilde x_i-(\tilde x_i^Tw)w\|^2=V_{\text{tot}}-w^TCw\quad(\|w\|=1)$$

Symbols aur assumptions:

  • $\tilde x=x-\mu$ — centred point ($\mu$ = data ka mean). Is page mein data $d\times n$ matrix $X$ ke columns hain ($d$ features, $n$ points) jab tak P9 jaisa $n\times d$ (rows = samples) na likha ho.
  • $W_k=[w_1\ \cdots\ w_k]\in\mathbb R^{d\times k}$ — top-$k$ PCs, orthonormal columns ($W_k^TW_k=I_k$, B7). $z_j$ — score (B8 G1).
  • $\hat x$ — reconstruction (centred coordinates mein); original coordinates mein $\hat x+\mu$. $r$ — residue. $\|\cdot\|$ — Euclidean norm.
  • $E_k$ — average squared reconstruction error, $1/n$ convention. $\lambda_j$, $V_{\text{tot}}$, CEV — B10 (J1–J3). $\sum_{j>k}$ — $j=k+1,\dots,d$.
  • L5: rank $C=$ nonzero eigenvalues ki ginti (B10 J5); centred data ke liye $\le n-1$. "Round" = ek PC nikalna aur uska projection subtract karna.
  • L6: har real number ek unit memory ($W_k$ ke $dk$ numbers + $n$ scores-vectors ke $kn$ numbers). Mean $\mu$ ($d$ numbers) aur $W_k$ ki orthonormality se bachi constraints gini nahi ja rahi (Deeper 5).
  • L7: single unit vector $w$ (line through origin), centred data; $V_{\text{tot}}=\tfrac1n\sum\|\tilde x_i\|^2$.

Words mein: L1 — point ko $k$ PCs par project karke wapas banao. L2 — jo bacha woh discarded directions ka hissa hai, kept directions ke perpendicular. L3 — residue ki squared length $=$ poori length$^2$ minus kept scores$^2$. L4 — sab points ka average loss $=$ discarded eigenvalues ka jod $=$ (kho gaya hissa) $\times$ total variance. L5 — loss zero tabhi jab sab nonzero eigenvalues rakh liye (rank tak). L6 — directions $+$ scores gino, raw se ghatao. L7 — ek line ke liye error $=$ total $-$ us line par variance.

Notice that… (1) L3 se L4 ek average ka fark hai: $\tfrac1n\sum_iz_{ij}^2=\lambda_j$ (B8 G5). (2) L2 mein $r$ ek vector hai, L3–L4 mein scalar (squared length). "Residues" maange to vector, "error" maange to scalar. (3) L6 mein $k$ sirf ek baar aata hai aur $d+n$ jod mein hai, product mein nahi; $dn$ raw hai. (4) L7 L3 ka $k=1$ wala, general line wala roop hai; $w=$ PC1 par minimum $V_{\text{tot}}-\lambda_1$.

Important insight: reconstruction error aur explained variance ek hi sikke ke do pehlu hain: $V_{\text{tot}}=\underbrace{\sum_{j\le k}\lambda_j}_{\text{captured}}+\underbrace{\sum_{j>k}\lambda_j}_{\text{error}}$. Isliye "error minimise karo" aur "variance maximise karo" ek hi optimisation hai (L7, Deeper 2).

This is why… PYQs mein "reconstruction error" ke sawaal ya to tail eigenvalues se, ya Pythagoras se, ya storage count se hal hote hain — kabhi data ke har point ka lamba calculation nahi chahiye.

⚙️ How it works — numbered mechanism

  1. Orientation padho: $X$ ka shape $d\times n$ hai ya $n\times d$ (rows = samples)? $k$ ya "rounds" ya rank kahan se aata hai?
  2. Reconstruction/residue ke sawaal: pehle centre karo ($\tilde x=x-\mu$), phir $z_j=w_j^T\tilde x$, $\hat x=\sum_{j\le k}z_jw_j$, $r=\tilde x-\hat x$. Original coordinates chahiye to $\hat x+\mu$.
  3. Dot test: har residue ko $w_j$ ($j\le k$) se dot karo; $0$ hona chahiye. Options eliminate karne ka sabse tez tareeka (P7 M3).
  4. Ek point ka error: $\|r\|^2=\|\tilde x\|^2-\sum_{j\le k}z_j^2$ (L3); residue vector banana zaroori nahi.
  5. Average error: eigenvalues diye hon to discarded $\lambda$'s ka jod (L4); $X^TX$ ke eigenvalues hon to $n$ se bhaago (B10 J4).
  6. Zero residue / rounds: rank gino — independent columns/features ya nonzero eigenvalues. Rounds ki ginti $=$ rank (L5). $d$ ya $n$ ko rank mat samjho.
  7. Storage: raw $dn$, compressed $k(d+n)$; ratio banao, $1-$ratio, $\times100$, round last mein (L6). Break-even check: $k<\tfrac{dn}{d+n}$.
  8. Statement/MSQ sawaal: har option ko ek label do — eigenvalue count, rounds, captured variance, reconstruct, ya storage — phir uska rule lagao (P1, P8).

🔬 Deeper insight (D3)

1. L2, L3: residue discarded directions mein hota hai — proof

$w_1,\dots,w_d$ ek orthonormal basis hain (B7), isliye $\tilde x=\sum_{j=1}^{d}z_jw_j$ (B8 G3). Pehle $k$ terms jod do ⇒ $\hat x$; $r=\tilde x-\hat x=\sum_{j>k}z_jw_j$ (L2). Kisi $w_l$ ($l\le k$) se dot: $w_l^Tr=\sum_{j>k}z_j\,w_l^Tw_j=0$ (orthonormal) ⇒ $r\perp$ kept PCs. Squared norm: $\|r\|^2=\sum_{j>k}z_j^2$ (cross terms $0$); aur $\|\tilde x\|^2=\sum_{j\le k}z_j^2+\sum_{j>k}z_j^2$ (G4) ⇒ L3. Example 3: $z_3^2=\tfrac{25}6$ aur $6-\tfrac43-\tfrac12=\tfrac{25}6$ ✓.

2. L7: error minimise karna = variance maximise karna

Ek unit $w$ ke liye (L3, $k=1$):

$$\|\tilde x-(\tilde x^Tw)w\|^2=\|\tilde x\|^2-(\tilde x^Tw)^2$$

Average lo:

$$E(w)=\tfrac1n\sum\|\tilde x_i\|^2-\tfrac1n\sum(\tilde x_i^Tw)^2=V_{\text{tot}}-w^TCw$$

$V_{\text{tot}} $w$ par depend nahi karta, isliye $E$ minimum wahi $w$ jo $w^TCw$ maximum kare (PC1, $\lambda_1$). Example: points $(\pm2,0),(0,\pm1)$: $V_{\text{tot}}=2.5$; $w=(1,0)$ par $E=0.5=2.5-2$, $w=(0,1)$ par $E=2.0=2.5-0.5$ (code-verified). P3 ka option 4 ($-\tfrac1n\sum(x^Tw)^2$) $E$ ka sirf woh hissa hai jo $w$ par depend karta hai — $-w^TCw$ — isliye error ke saath ek jaisa minimiser deta hai, par error nahi hai.

3. "Rounds": residue deflation aur rank

Round 1: $w_1$ nikalo, $r^{(1)}=\tilde x-(\tilde x^Tw_1)w_1$. Round 2: $r^{(1)}$ ke covariance ka top eigenvector $w_2$ ($w_2\perp w_1$ aata hai), $r^{(2)}=r^{(1)}-(r^{(1)T}w_2)w_2$, aur aage. Round $j$ ke baad $r^{(j)}=\sum_{l>j}z_lw_l$ (L2). $r^{(j)}=0$ $\iff$ $z_l=0$ sab $l>j$ ke liye sab points par $\iff\lambda_l=0$ ($l>j$) $\iff j\ge$ rank. Code: $\mathbb R^{30}$ mein 4-dim data par rounds ke baad max residue $5.68,\,3.76,\,1.51,\,8\times10^{-15}$ (4 rounds ke baad $\approx0$).

4. P9: columns ke relations centring ke baad bhi rehte hain

Agar $C_4=2C_1-C_2$ (as columns of $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times d}$) to centred columns $\tilde C_j=C_j-\bar C_j\mathbf 1$ ke liye bhi $\tilde C_4=2\tilde C_1-\tilde C_2$, kyunki average (mean) linear hai: $\bar C_4=2\bar C_1-\bar C_2$. To relations sahi rehte hain aur rank wahi: $\operatorname{rank}(\tilde X)=3$ jab $\tilde C_1,\tilde C_2,\tilde C_3$ independent hon (jo sawaal given karta hai). Rank-nullity: $6$ columns, $3$ independent relations (har relation ek naya column $C_4,C_5,C_6$ introduce karta hai ⇒ triangular, independent) ⇒ rank $=6-3=3$. Code: $150\times6$ matrix ka numeric rank $3$.

5. Storage count ki fine print

Exam convention: $k(d+n)$ numbers, mean aur orthonormality ignore (P5 ki key $5250=5(1000+50)$ exactly yahi hai; mean $+1000$ jodte to $6250$ aata, key se alag). P4 mein mean ($+d=20$) jodo to $5120$ ⇒ $74.4\%$, jo accepted range $74$–$75$ ke andar hai, isliye wahan dono convention chalte hain. Orthonormality ki wajah se $W_k$ ke asli free parameters $dk-\tfrac{k(k+1)}2$ hain, par yeh "kitne numbers store karne padte hain" ka exam sawaal nahi hai. Scores ko ek $k\times n$ matrix $Z=W_k^T\tilde X$ socho, directions $d\times k$: jodo $kn+dk$.

6. Original coordinates mein reconstruction

$\hat x_{\text{orig}}=\mu+W_kW_k^T(x-\mu)$. Residue $x-\hat x_{\text{orig}}=\tilde x-W_kW_k^T\tilde x$ wahi rehta hai (mean dono mein cancel). P7 mein $\mu=(1,1)$ PC1 ki line par hai, isliye uncentred points ko seedha $w_1$ par project karne se bhi wahi residues aate hain ($(0,0),(0.5,-0.5),(-0.5,0.5)$ — code-verified); general data mein centre karna zaroori hai.
