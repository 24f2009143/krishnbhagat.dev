🧭 Position — yeh kahan baitha hai

Peeche (prerequisites)Yeh subtopicAage (kya unlock hota hai)

🧠 Intuition — plain language

Ek seedhi line socho jo origin se guzarti hai, direction $w$ mein. Point $x$ se line par perpendicular giraao. Jahan woh line ko chhoota hai, woh point $p$ hai — line ka woh point jo $x$ ke sabse paas hai. Do cheezein poochi ja sakti hain:

  • Scalar projection (ek number): origin se $p$ tak kitna chale, line ki direction mein. Negative ho sakta hai agar $x$ ulti taraf jhuka ho.
  • Vector projection $p$ (ek vector): line par woh actual point.
  • Length of projection = $\|p\|$ = scalar projection ka absolute value.

Analogy [ADDED] — dopahar ki dhoop aur parchhai. Ek seedhi chhadi (vector $x$) zameen (line $w$) par tiki hai. Sooraj bilkul upar hai, roshni zameen ke perpendicular girti hai. Zameen par chhadi ki parchhai = projection. Chhadi zameen ke jitni paas jhuki ho, parchhai utni lambi; seedhi khadi ho (perpendicular) to parchhai zero.

Analogy kahan tootti hai: (1) Asli parchhai ka koi sign nahi, par scalar projection negative ho sakta hai ($x$ line ki ulti direction mein jhuka ho). (2) Asli roshni hamesha "neeche" girti hai; maths mein projection hamesha line ke perpendicular hota hai, chahe line kisi bhi angle par ho. (3) Higher dimensions ($\mathbb R^{100}$) mein "zameen" sirf ek line hai — picture nahi bana sakte, formula wahi rehta hai.

Yeh kya NAHI hai:

  • $x^Tw$ nahi — jab tak $\|w\|=1$ na ho. $w$ ko 2 se guna karo to $x^Tw$ double ho jaata hai, par line wahi rehti hai, isliye projection ki length bhi wahi rehni chahiye.
  • $\|x\|$ nahi — projection ki length hamesha $\le\|x\|$ hoti hai (parchhai chhadi se lambi nahi ho sakti, jab roshni perpendicular ho).
  • $x$ se line ki distance nahi — woh 1.2 ka reconstruction error hai (residual ki length).

📊 Visual intuition — stepwise panels (PYQ ka hi data)

📖 Formal version — Formula Protocol

$$\underbrace{\operatorname{comp}_w(x)=\frac{x^Tw}{\|w\|}=x^Tu}_{\text{scalar projection}}\qquad \underbrace{p=\operatorname{proj}_w(x)=\frac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}\,w=(x^Tu)\,u}_{\text{vector projection}}\qquad \underbrace{\|p\|=\frac{|x^Tw|}{\|w\|}}_{\text{length}}$$

Symbols aur assumptions:

  • $x\in\mathbb R^d$ — jis point / vector ko project karna hai.
  • $w\in\mathbb R^d$, $w\neq0$ — line ki direction. Line $=\{t\,w: t\in\mathbb R\}$, yaani origin se guzarti hai. $w$ ka unit hona zaroori nahi.
  • $u=w/\|w\|$ — same direction ka unit vector, $\|u\|=1$.
  • $x^Tw$ — dot product (B2-P2). $w^Tw=\|w\|^2$.
  • $p\in\mathbb R^d$ — line par woh point jo $x$ ke sabse paas hai.

Meaning in words: "$x$ ko $w$ ki unit direction ke saath dot karo — yahi line par chali hui doori hai. Us doori ko unit direction se guna karo — yahi line par point hai."

Notice that scalar formula mein $\|w\|$ ek baar divide hota hai, vector formula mein $\|w\|^2$ — kyunki vector formula mein $w$ khud bhi ek baar aur aata hai (ek $\|w\|$ us $w$ ko unit banane mein jaata hai). Dono ka matlab: "$w$ ko unit karo, phir kaam karo".

Small worked example. $x=(2,1),\ w=(4,0)$ (x-axis, non-unit). $x^Tw=2\cdot4+1\cdot0=8$, $\|w\|=4$. Scalar projection $=8/4=2$. $t^*=8/16=0.5$, $p=0.5\,(4,0)=(2,0)$. Length $\|p\|=2$ ✓ — x-axis par projection matlab bas $y$ ko 0 kar do, jo $(2,0)$ hi hai.

Important insight: projection sirf line par depend karta hai, $w$ ki length ya sign par nahi. $w$ ki jagah $c\,w$ ($c\neq0$) rakho: $\dfrac{x^T(cw)}{(cw)^T(cw)}\,(cw)=\dfrac{c^2\,x^Tw}{c^2\,w^Tw}\,w=p$ — $c^2$ cancel. Scalar projection ka sirf sign $c$ ke sign ke saath badal sakta hai.

This is why PCA mein PC ko unit-norm lete hain aur coordinate seedha $x^Tw_k$ likhte hain (B8): unit $w_k$ par scalar projection $=x^Tw_k$.

⚙️ How it works — mechanism

  1. Check: line origin se guzarti hai? ($w$ se defined line hamesha haan.) $w\neq0$?
  2. Dot: $x^Tw=\sum_j x_jw_j$.
  3. Norm: $\|w\|=\sqrt{w^Tw}$. Agar $\|w\|=1$ already, step 4 mein divide 1 se.
  4. Scalar projection: $x^Tw/\|w\|$. Question "length" maange to absolute value lo.
  5. (Agar vector chahiye) $t^*=x^Tw/w^Tw$, phir $p=t^*w$.
  6. Sanity: $|{\rm scalar}|\le\|x\|$ (Cauchy–Schwarz) aur $p$ ke components $w$ ke proportional.

Yeh line origin se nahi guzarti ($(0,1)$ par shuru hoti hai), isliye $\frac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}w$ seedha nahi lagta — pehle sab ko shift karna padta hai ($x-(0,1)$ project karo, phir $(0,1)$ wapas jodo). Isi wajah se PCA data ko pehle centre karta hai.

🔬 Deeper insight (D3) — formula kahan se aata hai, projection matrix, edge cases

1. Derivation: "sabse paas wala point"

Line ke har point ko $t\,w$ likh sakte hain. Hum woh $t$ chahte hain jo distance² minimum kare:

$$g(t)=\|x-tw\|^2=(x-tw)^T(x-tw)=x^Tx-2t\,x^Tw+t^2\,w^Tw.$$

(Middle term: $x^T(tw)+(tw)^Tx=2t\,x^Tw$, kyunki dot product symmetric hai — B2-P3.)

Derivative zero karo: $g'(t)=-2\,x^Tw+2t\,w^Tw=0\Rightarrow t^*=\dfrac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}$. Second derivative $g''(t)=2\,w^Tw>0$ (kyunki $w\neq0$), isliye yeh minimum hai. Isliye $p=t^*w$.

2. Residual hamesha perpendicular

$(x-p)^Tw=x^Tw-t^*\,w^Tw=x^Tw-\dfrac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}\,w^Tw=x^Tw-x^Tw=0$. Yaani $g'(t^*)=0$ ka geometric matlab hi "residual $\perp$ line" hai.

3. Projection matrix

$$P=\frac{ww^T}{w^Tw},\qquad p=Px.$$

Kyunki $Px=\dfrac{w\,(w^Tx)}{w^Tw}=\dfrac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}\,w$ (B2-P5 ki identity $(ww^T)x=w(w^Tx)$). Properties: $P^T=P$ (symmetric) aur $P^2=P$ (do baar project karna = ek baar). PYQ data par $P=\tfrac12\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$, $Px=\tfrac12(3+4,\ 3+4)=(3.5,\,3.5)$ ✓.

4. Bounds

Cauchy–Schwarz $|x^Tw|\le\|x\|\,\|w\|$ ⇒ $\|p\|=\dfrac{|x^Tw|}{\|w\|}\le\|x\|$. Barabari tabhi jab $x$ line par ho. Aur $\|p\|=\|x\|\,|\cos\theta|$ — B2-P4 ka shadow picture.

5. Edge cases

  • $x=0$ ⇒ $p=0$. $x\perp w$ ⇒ $p=0$ (scalar 0).
  • $w=0$ ⇒ line define nahi hoti; formula mein $0/0$.
  • Higher $d$: formulas bilkul same; sirf drawing nahi bana sakte.

1. 🔍 Decode

  • Asked: projection ki length — ek non-negative number, 2 decimals.
  • Given: $x=(3,4)$; line origin se, direction $w=(1,1)$. $w$ unit nahi hai: $\|w\|=\sqrt2$.
  • Trigger clues: "length of the projection", "line defined by $w$", $w$ ke components 1,1 (length 1 nahi).
  • Prerequisites: B2-P2 dot product (PACER: Procedural), B2-P3 norm / unit vector (Procedural), B2-P4 Pythagoras (Conceptual — Method 3 ke liye).
  • Core principle: $\|p\|=\dfrac{|x^Tw|}{\|w\|}$.

2. 🧭 Methods (3 genuinely different)

M1 General — unit vector se dot. M2 Advanced — pehle vector projection $p$, phir uska norm (yeh $p$ Q13 mein reuse hota hai). M3 Shortcut (sirf 2-D) — line ka normal vector lo, point se line ki distance nikalo, phir Pythagoras.

3. 🧮 Side-by-side working

M1 · Unit vector + dot

Step 1 (prepare): $\|w\|=\sqrt{1^2+1^2}=\sqrt2$. $u=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(1,1)$.

Step 2 (core): $x^Tu=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(3\cdot1+4\cdot1)=\tfrac{7}{\sqrt2}$.

Step 3 (number): $\tfrac{7}{\sqrt2}=\tfrac{7\sqrt2}{2}=\tfrac{7\times1.41421}{2}=4.9497$.

Step 4 (answer): length $=|4.9497|\approx\mathbf{4.95}$.

M2 · Vector projection, phir norm

Step 1 (prepare): $x^Tw=3+4=7$; $w^Tw=1+1=2$.

Step 2 (core): $t^*=\tfrac{7}{2}=3.5$; $p=3.5\,(1,1)=(3.5,\,3.5)$.

Step 3 (number): $\|p\|=\sqrt{3.5^2+3.5^2}=\sqrt{12.25+12.25}=\sqrt{24.5}=4.9497$.

Step 4 (answer): $\approx\mathbf{4.95}$.

M3 · Normal vector + Pythagoras

Step 1 (prepare): $(1,1)$ ke perpendicular $n=(1,-1)$ ($n^Tw=1-1=0$ ✓). $\|n\|=\sqrt2$. $\|x\|^2=9+16=25$.

Step 2 (core): point se line ki distance $d=\dfrac{|x^Tn|}{\|n\|}=\dfrac{|3-4|}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, $d^2=\tfrac12$.

Step 3 (number): Pythagoras: $\|p\|^2=\|x\|^2-d^2=25-0.5=24.5$, $\|p\|=\sqrt{24.5}=4.9497$.

Step 4 (answer): $\approx\mathbf{4.95}$.

📏 1.2 Projection-based reconstruction error

❓ Pretest — cold attempt

Context (Q11): $x=\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\end{bmatrix}$, line through the origin represented by $w=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$.

Calculate the magnitude/norm of the reconstruction error after projecting $x$ onto the line defined by $w$.

Enter the answer to two decimal places.

Upar ka 1.1 padh liya hai to bhi 1.2 ka theory padhne se pehle try karo. Confidence: S / U / G?

🧭 Position

PeecheYeh subtopicAage
1.1 projection $p$ · B2-P3 norm · B2-P4 Pythagoras Projection ke baad jo bacha (residual $r=x-p$) — kitna information kho gaya B5 PCA objective (error min ⇔ variance max) · B10 explained variance · B11 residues, "reconstruction error" MSQs · B17+ K-means objective bhi ek "reconstruction error" hai

🧠 Intuition

Socho tumhe $x=(3,4)$ kisi ko bhejna hai, par sirf ek number bhej sakte ho. Tum dono ne pehle se direction $u$ tay kar rakhi hai. Tum bhejte ho $x^Tu$ (scalar projection). Receiver point rebuild karta hai: $\hat x=(x^Tu)\,u=p$. Asli point aur rebuilt point ka farak $r=x-\hat x$ hi reconstruction error hai — jo information us ek number mein nahi aa paayi.

Analogy [ADDED] — sirf "kitna aage" batana. Kisi ko address batate ho: "highway par 4.95 km aage". Agar ghar highway ke bilkul kinare hai, perfect. Agar ghar highway se 0.71 km andar gali mein hai, to tumhari direction se woh highway par hi rukega — 0.71 km ka error. Woh 0.71 km highway ke perpendicular hai.

Kahan tootti hai: asli gali tedhi-medhi ho sakti hai; yahan "error" hamesha seedha, perpendicular hota hai — kyunki projection already sabse paas wala point chun leta hai. Aur asli duniya mein highway origin se guzarna zaroori nahi; yahan line origin se hi guzarti hai.

Yeh kya NAHI hai:

  • $\|x\|-\|p\|$ (lengths ka farak) nahi — vectors perpendicular jodte hain, lengths seedha subtract nahi hoti. PYQ par yeh $5-4.95=0.05$ deta, jo galat hai.
  • $\|x-w\|$ nahi — $w$ sirf direction hai, reconstructed point nahi.
  • "Norm of error" maangne par $\|r\|^2$ nahi — square root zaroori. (Dataset-level PCA formulas mein aksar squared error ka average hota hai — wording dhyaan se padho.)

📊 Visual intuition — direction badlo, error dekho

Upar ke stepwise panels ka Step 3 aur interactive explorer dono 1.2 bhi dikhate hain. Ab ek naya sawaal: agar $x=(3,4)$ fixed rakhein aur line ki direction ghumaayein, to error kaise badalta hai?

📖 Formal version — Formula Protocol

$$r=x-p=x-\frac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}\,w=x-(x^Tu)\,u,\qquad \|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-(x^Tu)^2=\|x\|^2-\frac{(x^Tw)^2}{w^Tw}$$

Symbols aur assumptions: $x,w,u,p$ jaise 1.1 mein; $w\neq0$, line origin se. $r$ = residual / reconstruction error vector; $\|r\|$ = uska norm ("magnitude of reconstruction error"); $\|r\|^2$ = squared error.

Meaning in words: "Error vector = asli point minus rebuilt point. Uski length² = poori length² minus jo hissa line ne pakad liya."

Derivation (koi step skip nahi): $r=x-(x^Tu)u$. Pehle dikhao $r\perp u$: $r^Tu=x^Tu-(x^Tu)(u^Tu)=x^Tu-(x^Tu)\cdot1=0$. Ab $x=p+r$ aur $p^Tr=(x^Tu)\,u^Tr=0$, isliye B2-P4 ka Pythagoras: $\|x\|^2=\|p\|^2+\|r\|^2$. Aur $\|p\|^2=(x^Tu)^2\,\|u\|^2=(x^Tu)^2$. Rearrange: $\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-(x^Tu)^2$.

Notice that formula ko $r$ vector banaye bina use kar sakte ho — sirf $\|x\|^2$ aur scalar projection chahiye. Isliye 1.1 ka answer seedha 1.2 mein jaata hai.

QuantityFormulaPYQ dataWording jo ise maangti hai

Error norm$\|r\|$$0.7071$"magnitude / norm of reconstruction error" — Q13

Dataset error$\tfrac1n\sum_i\|x_i-(x_i^Tw)w\|^2$—"reconstruction error for a dataset w.r.t. a line" (B11)

Small worked example. $x=(2,3)$, $w=(1,0)$ (unit). $x^Tu=2$, $p=(2,0)$, $r=(0,3)$, $\|r\|=3$. Formula se: $\|x\|^2-(x^Tu)^2=13-4=9$, $\sqrt9=3$ ✓. x-axis par project karne se $y$ ka poora hissa kho jaata hai.

Important insight: $0\le\|r\|\le\|x\|$. $\|r\|=0$ ⇔ $x$ line par hai. $\|r\|=\|x\|$ ⇔ $x\perp w$.

This is why "points ek $k$-dim subspace mein hain ⇒ $k$ PCs ke baad residue zero" (B11 PYQs): agar har point chuni hui directions ke span mein hai, to har $r_i=0$.

⚙️ How it works — mechanism

  1. 1.1 ke steps se scalar projection $s=x^Tw/\|w\|$ nikalo.
  2. Route A (vector): $p=(x^Tw/w^Tw)\,w$, $r=x-p$, $\|r\|=\sqrt{r^Tr}$.
  3. Route B (Pythagoras): $\|x\|^2$ nikalo, $\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-s^2$. $s^2$ ko exact fraction mein rakho ($49/2$, na ki $4.95^2$).
  4. Question "norm / magnitude" bole to square root; "squared" bole to nahi.
  5. Sanity: $r^Tw=0$ aur $\|p\|^2+\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2$.

🔬 Deeper insight (D3) — residual matrix, multiple directions, dataset-level = PCA

1. Residual bhi ek projection hai

$r=(I-P)x$ jahan $P=\dfrac{ww^T}{w^Tw}$. $(I-P)$ bhi symmetric aur idempotent hai: $(I-P)^2=I-2P+P^2=I-2P+P=I-P$. Yeh line ke perpendicular complement par projection hai. PYQ data: $(I-P)x=(3,4)-(3.5,3.5)=(-0.5,0.5)$.

2. Kai orthonormal directions

Agar $w_1,\dots,w_k$ orthonormal hain ($w_i^Tw_j=0$ for $i\neq j$, $\|w_i\|=1$), to reconstruction $\hat x=\sum_{j=1}^k(x^Tw_j)\,w_j$ aur

$$\|x-\hat x\|^2=\|x\|^2-\sum_{j=1}^{k}(x^Tw_j)^2.$$

Har orthonormal direction apna hissa alag se ghatati hai (Pythagoras baar-baar). Dhyaan: do directions ke "alag-alag errors" ka sum ($\|x-(x^Tw_1)w_1\|^2+\|x-(x^Tw_2)w_2\|^2$) yeh nahi hai — B11 mein isi type ka PYQ hai.

3. Dataset level ⇒ PCA

$n$ points ke liye average squared error, unit $u$ par:

$$\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-(x_i^Tu)u\|^2=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i\|^2-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i^Tu)^2=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i\|^2-u^TCu,$$

jahan $C=\tfrac1n\sum_ix_ix_i^T$. Beech ka step: $(x_i^Tu)^2=(u^Tx_i)(x_i^Tu)=u^T(x_ix_i^T)u$, phir average. Pehla term $u$ par depend nahi karta, isliye error minimise ⇔ $u^TCu$ maximise. Centred data par $u^TCu$ = $u$ direction mein variance. Yahi PCA hai (B5): best $u$ = $C$ ka top eigenvector.

Toy check [ADDED]. Centred points $(2,2),(-2,-2),(1,-1),(-1,1)$. Average $\|x_i\|^2=\tfrac{8+8+2+2}{4}=5$.
$u=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(1,1)$: projections $2\sqrt2,-2\sqrt2,0,0$ ⇒ variance $\tfrac{8+8+0+0}{4}=4$, error $=5-4=1$.
$u=(1,0)$: projections $2,-2,1,-1$ ⇒ variance $\tfrac{4+4+1+1}{4}=2.5$, error $=5-2.5=2.5$.
Har direction ke liye error + variance $=5$. Code-verified.

4. Centring kyun

Formula sirf origin se guzarti line ke liye hai. PCA "best fitting line" dhoondhta hai jo data ke mean se guzre; mean ko origin par laane ke liye pehle centre karte hain (B4).

📌 Memory card — T1 (1.1 + 1.2)

Core model: Line origin se, direction $w$. Projection = line ka sabse paas wala point; residual = jo bacha, hamesha line ke perpendicular.

Backbone chain: $w\to u=\tfrac{w}{\|w\|}\to s=x^Tu\to p=s\,u\to r=x-p\to\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-s^2\to$ dataset average $\to$ error min ⇔ $u^TCu$ max $\to$ PCA.

Formulas + conditions

  • $s=\dfrac{x^Tw}{\|w\|}$ (signed) · length $=|s|$ — $w\neq0$
  • $p=\dfrac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}w$ — kisi bhi nonzero $w$ ke liye
  • $\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-s^2$ — $u$ unit / $s$ sahi formula se
  • $P=\dfrac{ww^T}{w^Tw}$, $P^2=P$, $r=(I-P)x$
  • 2-D: $\|r\|=\dfrac{|x^Tn|}{\|n\|}$, $n\perp w$
  • Orthonormal $w_1..w_k$: $\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2-\sum_j(x^Tw_j)^2$

Top 3 traps

  1. $w$ normalise na karna ($7$ instead of $4.95$).
  2. Norm vs squared ($0.71$ vs $0.5$).
  3. Lengths subtract karna ($0.05$).

Trigger bank: "length of projection" → $|x^Tw|/\|w\|$ · "coordinate / representation" → $x^Tw_k$ (unit PC) · "residue" → $x-(x^Tw)w$ · "magnitude of reconstruction error" → $\sqrt{\|x\|^2-s^2}$.

Method selector

  • Sirf number (length / coordinate) → M1 unit + dot.
  • Aage residual bhi chahiye → M2 vector $p$ pehle.
  • Sirf error norm, koi bhi $d$ → Pythagoras ($s^2$ exact).
  • 2-D, sirf distance → normal vector.
  • Options mein residual vectors → $r^Tw=0$ se eliminate.
