🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

K-means kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]: data mein natural groups dhundhne ka sabse seedha tareeka — har point ko ek hi cluster (hard assignment), jahan cluster ka representative uska mean hai. Do kaam baari-baari: (1) har point ko nearest centre par bhejo, (2) har centre ko apne points ka mean bana do. Har kadam objective $F=\sum\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\|^2$ ko ghatata ya barabar rakhta hai.

Top 5 traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. $F$ ka argument / sum ka order — ek argument chhod dena, purane vs naye centres (4.3 P7), printed table / $\|x-y\|$ vs squared.
  2. 3rd centre mein nearest-of-all bhoolna — sirf $\mu_1$ se doori (B20 P2/P4, B21 P2/P6).
  3. Score rule na padhna — $D$ vs $D^2$, Euclid vs Manhattan (B21 P3 ki key $D$, P6 ki key $D^2$); "highest probability" ≠ "chuna jayega".
  4. Convergence conventions — terminating pass ka count, ties, iteration convention; ek pass ke baad bina verify ruk jana; "point gaya ⇒ centre badla".
  5. Converges ≠ optimal — kam $F$ ⇒ behtar clustering ($k=n$, $F=0$); har $z^{(0)}$ se wahi final; $\tfrac ab$ ulatna.
