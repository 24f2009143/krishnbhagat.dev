Tier 1 — general foundations

G1 · Vectors, Euclidean distance, squared norm

A. Core idea

Do points $x,y\in\mathbb R^d$ ke beech ki doori har coordinate ka fark nikaal ke milti hai. K-means mein squared distance chalta hai (root nahi): $\|x-y\|^2=\sum_{j=1}^d(x_j-y_j)^2$. Yeh $\ge0$ hai, aur $0$ tabhi jab $x=y$.

B. Notation and operations

  • $\|v\|^2=v^Tv=\sum_jv_j^2$ ($v$ ki apni lambai ka square).
  • Expand: $\|x-y\|^2=\|x\|^2-2x^Ty+\|y\|^2$ (L11 isi se nikalta hai).
  • 1-D mein $\|x-y\|^2=(x-y)^2$.

C. Worked examples

  • $x=(3,1)$, $y=(-1,0)$: $(3-(-1))^2+(1-0)^2=16+1=\mathbf{17}$.
  • $x=(4,0)$, $y=(-1,0)$: $(4+1)^2+0=\mathbf{25}$ — dhyaan do, $5$ nahi (P1 ka printed table yahin chook gaya).
  • $x=(3,1)$, $y=(2,0)$: $1+1=\mathbf2$.

D. Common mistakes

  • Fark ka square kiye bina jodna ($|4-(-1)|=5$ ko distance maan lena).
  • Coordinate-wise absolute values jodna ($|x_1-y_1|+|x_2-y_2|$ — yeh Manhattan hai; K-means++ ke kuch PYQs mein poochha gaya hai, par yahan nahi).
  • Squared aur normal distance mix karna: $\|x-y\|$ vs $\|x-y\|^2$.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: Squared distance lene se kaun sa centre "sabse paas" hai woh badalta hai? A: Nahi. Square non-negative numbers par increasing hai, to order wahi rehta hai. Badalta hai sirf $F$ ki value aur door ke points (outliers) ka asar — woh zyada chubhte hain.

G2 · Summation notation, argmin vs min

A. Core idea

$\min_k g(k)$ ek value hai (sabse chhoti). $\arg\min_k g(k)$ ek jagah hai (kaun sa $k$ us value par pahunchata hai). K-means mein assignment step ek argmin hai (kaun sa centre), update step bhi ek argmin hai (kaun sa point $v$).

B. Notation and operations

  • $\sum_{i\in C_k}$ = un $i$ par jod jo cluster $k$ mein hain. Poora $F$ do tareekon se likh sakte hain: $\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\|^2=\sum_{k=1}^K\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-\mu_k\|^2$ (point ke hisaab se ya cluster ke hisaab se — same cheez).
  • Tie: agar do $k$ barabar hon to argmin ek set hai; hum convention lete hain — chhota index (L5). Exams mein jahan tie ho, tie-break ka nuksaan niche P-solutions mein dikhaya hai.

C. Worked examples

  • $g(1)=5,\ g(2)=3,\ g(3)=3$: $\min=3$, $\arg\min=\{2,3\}$ ⇒ tie ⇒ $k=2$.
  • $z=(1,1,2,2)$, $x=(1,2,6,7)$, $\mu=(1.5,6.5)$: point-wise $0.25+0.25+0.25+0.25=1$; cluster-wise $(0.25+0.25)+(0.25+0.25)=1$ ✓.

D. Common mistakes

  • "argmin" par value likh dena (ya "min" par index).
  • Double sum mein index galat jodna: $\sum_k\sum_{i\in C_k}$ mein $i$ har point bas ek baar aata hai.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: L5 aur L6 ko ek-ek line mein kaise likhein? A: $z_i^{t+1}=\arg\min_k\|x_i-\mu_k^t\|^2$ aur $\mu_k^{t+1}=\arg\min_v\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-v\|^2$. Dono "argmin" hain — isi liye K-means ko "alternating minimisation" kehte hain; woh baad mein (4.4, B19) kaam aayega.

G3 · Mean (centroid) of vectors

A. Core idea

Points ka mean = har coordinate ka alag-alag average. Yeh "balance point" hai: agar har point par barabar wazan rakho to scale yahin santulit hota hai. Isi ko centroid kehte hain.

B. Notation and operations

  • $\mu=\frac1n\sum_ix_i$ coordinate-wise.
  • Deviations ka jod zero: $\sum_i(x_i-\mu)=0$ (yeh S1 ki proof ka heera hai).
  • Mean ek data point hona zaroori nahi.

C. Worked examples

  • $(2,2),(3,3),(4,4)$ ⇒ $\mu=(3,3)$.
  • $(1,2),(3,4),(5,9)$ ⇒ $\bigl(\tfrac{9}{3},\tfrac{15}{3}\bigr)=(3,5)$.
  • $\{1,2,6,7\}$ ke do halves ka mean: $1.5$ aur $6.5$ — koi data point nahi.

D. Common mistakes

  • Galat $n$ se divide karna (cluster ke size $n_k$ se, poore $n$ se nahi).
  • Mean ko median samajhna (1-D mein dono alag hote hain jab data skewed ho).
  • Cluster ke purane members par mean nikaalna jabki assignment badal chuka hai.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: K-means "means" kyun hai? A: kyunki update step mein har naya centre = us cluster ke current members ka mean. "Medoid" (data point hi chunna) ek alag algorithm hai.

Tier 2 — topic-specific maths

S1 · Mean hi sum of squared distances ka minimiser hai (L7, L9)

A. Core idea

Points $x_1,\dots,x_n$ ke liye $f(v)=\sum_i\|x_i-v\|^2$ ko kaun sa $v$ sabse chhota karta hai? Mean $\mu$. Aur kitna farak padta hai kisi aur $v$ par? Bilkul $n\|\mu-v\|^2$ — yeh "parallel-axis" jaisa identity hai.

B. Notation and operations

Identity (L7):

$$\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-v\|^2=\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-\mu\|^2+n\,\|\mu-v\|^2.$$

Proof, koi step chhoote bina. $x_i-v=(x_i-\mu)+(\mu-v)$. Square karo:

$$\|x_i-v\|^2=\|x_i-\mu\|^2+2(x_i-\mu)^T(\mu-v)+\|\mu-v\|^2.$$

$i$ par jodo. Beech ka term: $2\bigl(\sum_i(x_i-\mu)\bigr)^T(\mu-v)=2\cdot0^T(\mu-v)=0$ (G3 ka fact). Aakhri term $n$ baar aata hai. Isse identity mil gayi. Kyunki $n\|\mu-v\|^2\ge0$ aur $=0$ sirf $v=\mu$ par, minimiser sirf $\mu$ hai.

Dusra tareeka (calculus): $\nabla_vf=-2\sum_i(x_i-v)=0\Rightarrow v=\frac1n\sum_ix_i=\mu$; Hessian $=2nI\succ0$ ⇒ minimum.

Scaled version (L9): $\sum_i\|ax_i-bv\|^2=b^2\sum_i\|\tfrac abx_i-v\|^2$ ($b\ne0$). Yeh $\tfrac ab x_i$ points ke mean ko minimiser banata hai: $v^*=\tfrac ab\mu$.

C. Worked examples

  • 1-D $\{0,2,10\}$: $\mu=4$. $f(4)=16+4+36=56$. $f(2)=4+0+64=68$, aur $56+3(4-2)^2=68$ ✓.
  • 2-D: $\{(0,0),(4,0)\}$, $\mu=(2,0)$, $f(\mu)=4+4=8$; $v=(0,0)$ par $f=0+16=16=8+2\cdot4$ ✓.

D. Common mistakes

  • "Median minimiser hai" — median $\sum|x_i-v|$ ka minimiser hai; squared ke liye mean.
  • Scaling ko idhar-udhar karna: $\arg\min\sum\|3x_i-2v\|^2$ mein answer $\tfrac32\mu$ hai, $\mu$ nahi, aur $\tfrac23\mu$ bhi nahi (P3).

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: Update step mean hi kyun hai, koi aur formula kyun nahi? A: Assignment $z$ fixed rakhte hue $F$ sirf $\mu_k$'s par depend karta hai, aur har cluster ka hissa $\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-\mu_k\|^2$ hai. S1 kehta hai ki iska best $\mu_k$ cluster ka mean hai. Yani update step $F$ ko us $z$ ke liye sabse zyada ghatata hai.

S2 · Assignments, clusters aur objective $F(z,\mu)$ (L2–L4)

A. Core idea

K-means ek labelling $z=(z_1,\dots,z_n)$ aur ek centres ki list $\mu=(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_K)$ rakhta hai. $F(z,\mu)=\sum_i\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\|^2$ batata hai ki labelling aur centres milke data ko kitni achhi tarah "jodte" hain. Yeh do alag cheezon ka function hai — isliye "F kya hai?" ka answer tab tak adhoora hai jab tak $z$ aur $\mu$ dono na pata hon.

B. Notation and operations

  • $F(z,\mu)$ ka kaam: har point ko apne label wale centre se naapo.
  • Jab $\mu_k$ cluster $k$ ka mean hai, to $F$ ka naam "within-cluster sum of squares" (SSE) hai. Shortcut (L11): $\sum_{i\in C}\|x_i-\mu\|^2=\sum_{i\in C}\|x_i\|^2-n_C\|\mu\|^2$. Proof: L7 mein $v=0$ rakho.

C. Worked examples

  • 1-D $x=(1,2,6,7)$, $z=(1,1,2,2)$. $\mu=(1,7)$: $F=0+1+1+0=2$. $\mu=(1.5,6.5)$: $F=1$. Wahi $z$, alag $\mu$ ⇒ alag $F$.
  • Wahi $\mu=(1,7)$ par alag $z=(2,2,1,1)$: $F=36+25+25+36=122$.

D. Common mistakes

  • "After assigning, find the objective" ko bina soche kisi ek $\mu$ se jodna. Hamesha likho: $F(z^{\text{kaun sa}},\mu^{\text{kaun sa}})$.
  • $F$ ko sirf $\mu$ ka function samajhna (jaise $F=\min_z\ldots$) — woh tab sahi jab har point ko nearest centre se jodo.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: Ek iteration mein $F$ ki value kitni baar aur kaise badalti hai? A: Teen "pad" hote hain: $F(z^t,\mu^t)$ → (assign) $F(z^{t+1},\mu^t)$ → (update) $F(z^{t+1},\mu^{t+1})$. Har tir ke beech ek hi argument badalta hai (L8). P1 aur P2 yahi pad pooch rahe hain.

S3 · Nearest-centroid regions: half-planes aur convex cells (L12)

A. Core idea

Do centres $a,b$ ke liye woh saare $x$ jo $a$ ke zyada paas hain, $a$–$b$ ki perpendicular bisector ke ek taraf aate hain (ek half-plane). $K$ centres ke liye cluster $k$ ka region $K-1$ half-planes ka intersection hai ⇒ convex polygon (Voronoi cell).

B. Notation and operations

$\|x-a\|^2\le\|x-b\|^2$. Dono expand karo: $\|x\|^2-2x^Ta+\|a\|^2\le\|x\|^2-2x^Tb+\|b\|^2$. $\|x\|^2$ dono taraf kat gaya ⇒ $2x^T(b-a)\le\|b\|^2-\|a\|^2$. Yeh $x$ mein linear hai ⇒ half-plane.

C. Worked examples

  • $a=(0,0)$, $b=(4,0)$: $8x_1\le16\Rightarrow x_1\le2$. Bisector $x_1=2$.
  • $a=(-1,0)$, $b=(2,0)$: $2x_1\cdot3\le4-1=3\Rightarrow x_1\le0.5$. Check: $(0.5,5)$ par dono distances$^2$ $=2.25+25$ ✓ (barabar, bisector par).

D. Common mistakes

  • Cells ko gol (circles) maan lena — nahi, seedhi-sidhi polygons.
  • Do clusters ke beech ki seemaa ko "data ke beech ka gap" maan lena — seemaa centres ki bisector hoti hai, data ki nahi.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: Concentric rings ko K-means alag kyun nahi kar sakta? A: Cells convex hain, ring "andar + bahar" ek convex region mein nahi aati. (4.3, B18 mein iske PYQs hain; yahan sirf neev rakhi.)

S4 · Monotone decrease + finite set ⇒ loop ruk jaata hai (L8)

A. Core idea

Agar ek sankhya ka sequence kabhi badhe nahi, aur kuch se neeche bhi na jaa sake ($F\ge0$), to woh ruk-ruk ke ghatega. Aur agar sambhav "states" bas finite hon (n points ko $K$ groups mein baantne ke ek finite tareeke, $\le K^n$), aur har asli badlav par $F$ strictly ghatta hai, to koi state do baar nahi aa sakti ⇒ algorithm finite steps mein ruk jaata hai.

B. Notation and operations

  • Assignment step: har point ko nearest centre par bhejo ⇒ $F(z^{t+1},\mu^t)\le F(z^t,\mu^t)$ (har point ki apni cost ghati ya wahi rahi).
  • Update step: $\mu^{t+1}$ = naye clusters ke means ⇒ S1 se $F(z^{t+1},\mu^{t+1})\le F(z^{t+1},\mu^t)$.
  • Dono jodke L8. Gap exact: $F(z,\mu^{\text{old}})-F(z,\mu^{\text{new}})=\sum_kn_k\|\mu_k^{\text{old}}-\mu_k^{\text{new}}\|^2$ (L7 se).

C. Worked examples

  • Data $\{0,2,4,9,10\}$, $\mu^0=(0,5)$: $F$ ki kadi $46\to22.67\to18.22\to8.50\to8.50\to8.50$ — kabhi nahi badhi (4.1 Example 2 mein poori table).

D. Common mistakes

  • "F ghatta hai ⇒ global minimum milta hai" — nahi. Sirf local minimum / fixed point tak pahunchta hai (initialisation par nirbhar, 4.5).
  • "F strictly ghatta hai har iteration" — nahi; jab assignment nahi badla to $F$ wahi rehta hai aur algorithm ruk jaata hai. Tie par $F$ bina ghate bhi assignment badal sakta hai.

E. Why K-means needs this

Q: "K-means ek hi partition do baar dekh sakta hai kya?" A: Nahi: do baar dekhne ka matlab $F$ wapis usi value par, yaani beech mein kahin badhna ya barabar rehna padega. Barabar rehne par assignment nahi badla ⇒ ruk gaye. Yeh P8 ka argument hai.

  • $F$ ek "kharcha" hai: har point ke centre se doori$^2$ ka jod. Dono kaam is kharche ko kam karte hain (ya wahi rehne dete hain). Isiliye loop ke aakhir mein shaanti hoti hai.
  • $F$ ke do haath hain: labels $z$ aur centres $\mu$. Pehla kaam labels badalta hai (centres wahi), doosra centres badalta hai (labels wahi). Isliye "F" ki value kaun sa waqt hai uspe depend karti hai.
  • Centre data point nahi hota. Mean do data points ke beech khali jagah par ho sakta hai ($\{1,2\}$ ka mean $1.5$).

📖 Formal version — Formula Protocol

$$F(z,\mu)\;=\;\sum_{i=1}^{n}\bigl\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\bigr\|^2\;=\;\sum_{k=1}^{K}\sum_{i\in C_k}\bigl\|x_i-\mu_k\bigr\|^2 .$$
  • Symbols: $x_i\in\mathbb R^d$ data; $z_i\in\{1,\dots,K\}$ point $i$ ka label; $C_k=\{i:z_i=k\}$ cluster $k$ ke point; $\mu_k\in\mathbb R^d$ cluster $k$ ka centre; $\|\cdot\|$ Euclidean norm.
  • Assumptions: $K\le n$; $z$ aur $\mu$ do alag, swatantra cheezein hain jinhe hum jaise chahein chun sakte hain; sirf Lloyd's algorithm mein $\mu$ ko "mean of $z$" bana dete hain.
  • Words mein: har point ke apne label waale centre se doori$^2$ ka jod.

⚙️ How it works — ek iteration, no skipped steps

  1. Input: points $x_1..x_n$, current centres $\mu_1..\mu_K$ ($\mu^0$ diya hua ya chuna hua).
  2. Distance table: har point $i$ aur har centre $k$ ke liye $d_{ik}=\|x_i-\mu_k\|^2$ (G1 ke formula se).
  3. Assign: har row ka $\min$ lo, uska column index $z_i$ (tie par chhota index).
  4. F after assign: $F(z,\mu^t)=\sum_i\min_kd_{ik}$ (har row ka minimum jodo).
  5. Update: har cluster $k$ ke members ka coordinate-wise mean $\to\mu_k^{t+1}$. (Khaali cluster ho to purana centre rakhte hain — convention.)
  6. F after update: naye centres se ek aur distance pass — $F(z,\mu^{t+1})$.
  7. Check: agar assignment pichhli baar jaisa hi nikla ya $\mu$ nahi badla ⇒ ruk jao. Warna step 2 par wapas.

🔬 Deeper insight (D3) — proofs, edge cases, connections

1. Gap identity (L7 se, har step likha hua)

$z$ fixed. Cluster $k$ ke liye $v=\mu_k^{\text{old}}$ aur "mean" $=\mu_k^{\text{new}}$ lo (naya centre = cluster ka mean). L7 se $\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-\mu_k^{\text{old}}\|^2=\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-\mu_k^{\text{new}}\|^2+n_k\|\mu_k^{\text{new}}-\mu_k^{\text{old}}\|^2$. $k$ par jodo: $F(z,\mu^{\text{old}})-F(z,\mu^{\text{new}})=\sum_kn_k\|\mu_k^{\text{old}}-\mu_k^{\text{new}}\|^2\ge0$, aur $=0$ sirf jab har cluster ka centre wahi rahe.

2. Assign step kyun $F$ nahi badhata

$F(z,\mu^t)=\sum_i\|x_i-\mu^t_{z_i}\|^2$ ke har term mein sirf $z_i$ badalta hai. $z_i^{t+1}$ us $k$ ko chunta hai jo $\|x_i-\mu_k^t\|^2$ sabse chhota kare ⇒ har term $\le$ purane term ⇒ jod $\le$ purana jod. Barabar sirf tab jab koi point nahi badla (ya tie).

3. Edge cases

  • Khaali cluster: kisi centre ko koi point na mile to mean undefined ($0/0$). Convention: purana centre rakho, ya naya random chuno. Yeh bhi $F$ nahi badhata.
  • $K=n$ ya $K\ge$ distinct points: $F$ $0$ tak jaa sakta hai (har point apna centre). Isliye "chhota $F$" akela $K$ chunne ka criterion nahi (4.6).
  • Scale: data ko $c$ se guna karo to $F$ $c^2$ guna ho jaata hai par clusters wahi (distances ka order same).

4. L11 (SSE shortcut) ka proof

L7 mein $v=0$: $\sum\|x_i\|^2=\sum\|x_i-\mu\|^2+n\|\mu\|^2\Rightarrow\sum\|x_i-\mu\|^2=\sum\|x_i\|^2-n\|\mu\|^2$.

5. Cost aur connection

Ek iteration $O(nKd)$ (har point ke liye $K$ distances, har distance $O(d)$). Dual PCA ($K=XX^T$, B12) aur K-means dono mein $\|x_i-x_j\|^2=x_i^Tx_i-2x_i^Tx_j+x_j^Tx_j$ ka dot-product structure dikhta hai — kernel K-means isi par bana hai (syllabus ke bahar).

⚙️ How it works — "converged means chahiye" waale sawaal ka recipe

  1. Likho: data, $\mu^0$, $K$.
  2. Pass 1 assign: har point ka nearest centre (distance$^2$, tie ⇒ chhota index).
  3. Pass 1 update: har cluster ke members ka mean ⇒ $\mu^1$.
  4. Pass 2 assign: $\mu^1$ se dobara nearest. Agar koi point label badle to cluster ke naye members ke saath update (step 3 dobara), warna ruk jao.
  5. Fast-path: pass 1 ke baad data ke shape se andaaza lagao (sabse zyada separated groups), un groups ke means nikaalo, phir verify karo ki har point apne mean ke paas hai (fixed point). Par yeh bhi confirm karo ki given $\mu^0$ se pass 1 wahi groups deta hai (varna alag fixed point ho sakta hai).
  6. New point sawaal: naya point nearest centre ko do; sirf us cluster ka mean L10 se update karo; ek aur pass se check karo ki koi bhi point label nahi badla.

🔬 Deeper insight (D3) — incremental formula ka proof, SSE par asar, online vs batch

1. Incremental mean ka proof

$n\mu=\sum_{i=1}^nx_i$. Naya point $x$: $\mu^{\text{new}}=\dfrac{n\mu+x}{n+1}=\dfrac{(n+1)\mu+(x-\mu)}{n+1}=\mu+\dfrac{x-\mu}{n+1}$. Hatne par: $\mu^{\text{new}}=\dfrac{n\mu-x}{n-1}$ ($n\ge2$).

2. SSE par asar (L7 se, no step skipped)

Purana $S=\sum_{i=1}^n\|x_i-\mu\|^2$. Naya mean $\mu'$, naya set mein $n+1$ points. L7 mein $v=\mu$ aur "mean" $=\mu'$ lo (naye set ke liye): $\sum_{\text{naye}}\|x_j-\mu\|^2=S'+(n+1)\|\mu'-\mu\|^2$. Left side $=S+\|x-\mu\|^2$ (purane points ka jod $S$, plus naya). Aur $\|\mu'-\mu\|^2=\tfrac{\|x-\mu\|^2}{(n+1)^2}$ (incremental formula se). To $S'=S+\|x-\mu\|^2-\tfrac{\|x-\mu\|^2}{n+1}=S+\tfrac n{n+1}\|x-\mu\|^2$. Example 5: $11+\tfrac45\cdot2.25=12.8$ ✓.

3. Naye point se baaki cluster ke labels kyun flip ho sakte hain

Cluster 2 ka centre hil gaya ⇒ doosre points ke liye "nearest" badal sakta hai. Jab centre $O(\tfrac1{n+1})$ khiske to flip kam hote hain par zero nahi — isliye ek pass se verify.

4. Batch (Lloyd) vs online

Lloyd poora data assign karke tab means badalta hai. "Online/sequential" K-means ek-ek point par centre ko L10 se khiskata hai — alag algorithm; PYQs mein hamesha batch hai jab tak sawaal na likhe.

5. Fixed point $\ne$ global minimum

Converged $(\mu,z)$ aisa hai ki na labels badal sakte hain, na centre; par kisi aur shuruaat se aur chhota $F$ mil sakta hai (4.5 mein: K-means++).
