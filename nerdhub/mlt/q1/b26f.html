2 · Consolidated cheat sheet

T1 · Linear Algebra

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Scalar projection of $x$ on $w$$\dfrac{x^Tw}{\|w\|}$Signed length. Unit $w$: $x^Tw$.
Vector projection$\dfrac{x^Tw}{w^Tw}w$Denominator $\|w\|^2$ (square!).
Residual$r=x-\text{proj}$, $r^Tw=0$Hamesha direction se orthogonal.
Squared reconstruction error$\|x\|^2-\dfrac{(x^Tw)^2}{w^Tw}$Pythagoras.
Squared distance$\|x-y\|^2=\|x\|^2-2x^Ty+\|y\|^2$K-means, kernels, GMM sab jagah.

T2 · PCA

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Centre + covariance$x_i\leftarrow x_i-\mu$; $C=\frac1nXX^T$[PATCHED] $1/n$ confirmed (F6/F7). $\frac1{n-1}$ ho to eigenvalues $\frac n{n-1}$ guna.
PCs$Cw=\lambda w,\ \|w\|=1$; PC$_k$ = $k$-th largest $\lambda$$C$ symmetric PSD; eigenvectors orthogonal; $\pm w$ dono valid.
Variance along unit $w$$w^TCw$; max $=\lambda_1$Unit-norm zaroori. Cube $\{\pm1\}^3$: har direction par $1$.
Score$x^Tw_k$Centred $x$.
Explained ratio$\dfrac{\sum_{j\le k}\lambda_j}{\sum_j\lambda_j}$Total variance $=\text{tr}(C)$ (F7: $\lambda_1\ne$ total).
Rank-$k$ error$\sum_{j>k}\lambda_j$ (avg squared, $1/n$)Data $k$-dim subspace mein ⇒ $\le k$ nonzero eigenvalues; $k$ PCs par residue $0$.
Rank bound$\text{rank}(C)\le\min(d,n-1)$Centring se ek rank kam.
Dual PCA ($d>n$)$X^TXv=\nu v$; $w=\dfrac{Xv}{\sqrt\nu}$; eig($C$)$=\nu/n$$XX^T$ aur $X^TX$ ke nonzero eigenvalues same; eigenvectors alag.
Storage$k(d+n)$ vs $dn$[PATCHED] Mean store nahi (5250; 74.5%).

T3 · Kernels

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Kernel, Gram$k=\phi(x)^T\phi(y)$; $K_{ij}=k(x_i,x_j)$Linear: $K=X^TX$.
Valid kernel$K$ symmetric aur PSDSirf symmetric kaafi nahi.
Polynomial / Gaussian$(x^Ty+c)^p,\ c\ge0$; $e^{-\|x-y\|^2/2\sigma^2}$Gaussian: $k(x,x)=1$. $(1+x^Ty)^4$ at $(1,-1),(2,1)$: $16$ ✓.
Closure$k_1+k_2,\ ak_1\ (a\ge0),\ k_1k_2,\ f(x)f(y),\ e^{k_1}$ valid$k_1-k_2$, $-k_1$ nahi; $|x-y|$ nahi.
Feature count$(x^Ty+c)^p$ on $\mathbb R^d$: $\binom{d+p}{p}$[PATCHED] $d=4,p=3$: $35$ ✓.
Centred Gram$K^c=K-\mathbf1_nK-K\mathbf1_n+\mathbf1_nK\mathbf1_n$Kernel PCA se pehle.
Kernel PCA$K^cv=\nu v$; variance $\nu/n$; score $\sqrt{\nu_k}v_k[i]$Linear kernel ⇒ ordinary PCA. Components $d$ se zyada ho sakte hain.

T4 · K-means

ItemFormula / ruleCondition / note
Objective$F=\sum\|x_i-\mu_{z_i}\|^2$Squared distance.
LloydAssign (argmin), update (plain mean)Pehle sab assign, tab update.
Monotonicity$F$ non-increasing har stepFinite steps; local minimum; init par depend (7.5).
K-means++$P(x)=\dfrac{D(x)^2}{\sum D^2}$[PATCHED] Default $D^2$; 2025 Feb Q9 plain $D$. Chosen point ki prob $0$.
Choosing $K$$F$ $K$ ke saath ghatta hai; $K=n$ par $0$Sirf $F$ se $K$ nahi.
$\arg\min_v\sum\|ax_i-bv\|^2$$v^*=\frac ab\bar x$2026 Jul Q2: $\frac32\bar x$.

T5 · Probability, MLE, Bayes

ItemFormulaCondition / note
MLE recipe$\ell=\sum\ln f$; $\ell'=0$i.i.d.
Bernoulli$\hat p=\#\text{ones}/n$$p=P(x=1)$.
Gaussian$\hat\mu=\bar x$, $\hat\sigma^2=\frac1n\sum(x-\bar x)^2$$n$ se divide.
Poisson$\hat\lambda=\bar x$Freq table: $\sum xf/\sum f$ ($2.52$ ✓).
Exponential$\hat\lambda=n/\sum x$pdf $\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$.
Pareto ($x_m=1$)$\hat\theta=n/\sum\ln x_i$[PATCHED] pdf $\theta x^{-\theta-1}$, $x\ge1$: $1.1485$ ✓ (key $1.147$–$1.150$).
Beta–BernoulliBeta$(\alpha+h,\beta+t)$Mean $\frac\alpha{\alpha+\beta}$; mode $\frac{\alpha-1}{\alpha+\beta-2}$ ($\alpha,\beta>1$).

T6 · Convexity

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Convex$f(\lambda a+(1-\lambda)b)\le\lambda f(a)+(1-\lambda)f(b)$$f''\ge0$.
Jensen$f(\sum\lambda_ja_j)\le\sum\lambda_jf(a_j)$$\lambda_j\ge0$, $\sum=1$ (negative ya sum$\ne1$ ✗).
Log version$\ln(\sum\lambda_ja_j)\ge\sum\lambda_j\ln a_j$$\ln$ concave; EM ke $\ell$ ka bound.
Local vs globalConvex ⇒ local $=$ globalRegression MSE convex; GMM $\ell$ nahi.

T7 · GMM + EM

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Mixture density$p(x)=\sum\pi_k\mathcal N(x|\mu_k,\sigma_k^2)$$\pi\ge0$, $\sum\pi=1$.
Log-likelihood$\sum_i\ln\sum_k\pi_kf_k(x_i)$Sum ke andar $\ln$ nahi todta ⇒ EM.
E-step$\gamma_k^i=\dfrac{\pi_kf_k(x_i)}{\sum_j\pi_jf_j(x_i)}$$\gamma\in[0,1]$, $\sum_k\gamma_k^i=1$. 2 comps: $\gamma_1=\sigma(\ln\text{odds})$.
M-step$N_k=\sum\gamma$; $\pi_k=N_k/n$; $\mu_k=\sum\gamma x/N_k$; $\sigma_k^2=\sum\gamma(x-\mu_k)^2/N_k$[PATCHED] Naya $\mu_k$ use; spherical $d$-dim: denominator $dN_k$.
Ratios$\gamma_1/\pi_1=f(x|1)/p(x)$; $\sum_{k\ge2}\gamma_k=1-\gamma_1$$p(x)$ do numbers se nahi (2026 Jul Q9).
EM vs LloydSoft vs hard; $\pi,\sigma^2$ bhi seekhta; $\gamma\to\{0,1\}$ ⇒ Lloyd$\ell\uparrow$, $F\downarrow$; dono local, init-sensitive, $K$-sensitive.

T8 · Regression

ItemFormulaCondition / note
Model, loss$\hat y=w^Tx$; MSE $=\frac1n\sum(w^Tx_i-y_i)^2$Bias nahi (course). Sum $=n\cdot$MSE.
Gradient$\frac2nX(X^Tw-y)$Sum loss ka $2X(\cdot)$.
Closed form$XX^Tw=Xy$; $w^*=(XX^T)^{-1}Xy$Invertible chahiye. $L\ge0$: exact fit ⇒ MSE $0$ ⇒ best fit.
GD$w\leftarrow w-\eta\nabla$[PATCHED] $0<\eta<2/L$, $L=\frac2n\lambda_{\max}(XX^T)$; $\eta=2/L$ oscillation; $>2/L$ diverge. Rate $\max|1-\eta\lambda_j|$.
SGDEk point ka gradientConstant $\eta$ par noise-floor; global-min "guaranteed" ✗; exact-fit data par converge.

4 · Top traps (topic-wise)

  • T1: $\|w\|^2$ se divide; non-unit $w$ ko unit maan lena.
  • T2: Centring bhoolna; $\frac1n$ vs $\frac1{n-1}$; $\lambda_1$ vs total variance; storage mein mean ginna.
  • T3: "symmetric ⇒ valid" ✗; $k_1-k_2$; kernel PCA se pehle centring; $d$ se zyada components.
  • T4: $D$ vs $D^2$; ek ek point par update; $F$ se $K$ chunna; converge $\ne$ global optimum.
  • T5: MLE variance $\frac1n$; $p=P(x=1)$ ka direction; Beta mean vs mode; prior ke $\alpha,\beta$ bhoolna.
  • T6: Jensen mein weights non-negative + sum $1$; concave par ulti inequality.
  • T7: Hard count ($\gamma\ge0.5$) vs soft weight; naya $\mu$ vs purana $\mu$ $\sigma^2$ mein; EM ke $\ell\uparrow$ ko global max samajhna.
  • T8: $X^TX$ vs $XX^T$; sum vs mean; "$\eta$ koi bhi"; SGD "guaranteed"; $\eta=2/L$ ko convergent maanna.
