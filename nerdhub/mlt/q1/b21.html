📘 4.5 K-means++ — part B ka naya theory

🔁 Recall warm-up [ADDED]

Pehle khud likho, phir "Show answer" kholo. (B20, B19, B17 se.)

R1 (B20). Centres $(0,0)$ aur $(10,0)$ chune ja chuke hain. Point $(9,0)$ ka 3rd-centre score (squared rule) kya hoga — $81$ ya kuch aur?

Show answer

$D=\min(9,\,1)=1$ (L25: nearest of all chune hue), score $=D^2=1$, $81$ nahi. Sirf $\mu_1$ se doori lene par point ko ghalat bada score mil jaata.

R2 (B19). Lloyd ke assign step aur update step — dono mein $F$ kaise badalta hai, aur kyun?

Show answer

Dono mein $F$ badhta nahi. Assign: har point sabse paas wale centre par jaata hai ⇒ uski $\|x-\mu\|^2$ ghatti ya barabar. Update: mean $\sum\|x-v\|^2$ ka minimiser hai (B17), isliye naya centre purane se kharab nahi. Partitions finite hain ⇒ koi configuration dobara nahi aati (L19–L22).

R3 (B17). $v^*=\arg\min_v\sum_i\|4x_i-3v\|^2$, jahan $x_i$ ka mean $(3,6)$ hai. $v^*$ kya hai?

Show answer

$\sum\|4x_i-3v\|^2=9\sum\|\tfrac43x_i-v\|^2$, jo $v=\tfrac43\cdot\text{mean}(x)$ par minimum hota hai ⇒ $v^*=\tfrac43(3,6)=(4,8)$. Check: gradient $\sum -6(4x_i-3v)=0\Rightarrow v=\tfrac43\bar x$ ✓.

🧭 Position — part A ke baad kya naya

  • Kahan hai: Lloyd ke shuru ka kadam. Part A (B20) mein engine ($D$, score, $s/\sum s$, nearest-of-all, chuna hua ⇒ $0$) aur do dataset-types (1-D line, collinear points) the. Part B uska variations wala hissa hai.
  • Prerequisites: L23–L28 (B20), Lloyd ka loop aur local fixed point (L17–L22), $D^2$ aur Euclid distance (L5).
  • Aage kya khulta hai: T4 ka timed set (is page ke end mein), phir T5 (probability: MLE, Bernoulli). K-means++ mein "weights ⇒ probabilities" ki jo aadat bani, wahi T5 mein likelihood ke saath kaam aati hai.

🧠 Intuition — plain language

K-means++ ek lottery hai: har point ko uske score jitne tickets milte hain, ek ticket nikalta hai, uska point agla centre ban jaata hai. Part A ne dikhaya ki door wale points ke zyada tickets hote hain. Part B ke teen naye sawaal:

  1. Tickets kitne? Standard rule mein $D^2$ tickets — doori double ⇒ tickets 4 guna. Kuch sawaal (aur unki keys) sirf $D$ tickets maante hain — doori double ⇒ sirf 2 guna. Winner wahi rehta hai, par probability ka number badal jaata hai (WE4).
  2. Ticket-book har baar naya: jaise hi koi naya centre chuna jaata hai, har point ka $D$ (aur tickets) ghat sakte hain, badh nahi sakte (min liya hai). Isliye ranking flip ho sakti hai (WE5) — pehle jo sabse zyada door tha ab sabse kam ho sakta hai.
  3. Tie: jab koi point do centres se barabar doori par ho, to Lloyd ko batana padta hai ki woh kidhar jaaye. Alag tie-break ⇒ alag final centres, aur kabhi alag $F$ bhi (P5, WE6).

Analogy (labelled, [ADDED]): ghar ke paas kitne dukaan-delivery ke "zone" hain — naya warehouse khulte hi har gali ka "sabse paas wala warehouse" ghat sakta hai, badh nahi sakta. Kahan toot-ti hai: asli K-means++ mein warehouse ki jagah sirf existing data points hoti hain, koi bhi jagah nahi.

Yeh kya NAHI hai: "highest probability" ka matlab "wahi chuna jaayega" nahi hai (sampling hai). Sirf C6 jaise sawaal jo saaf likhte hain "highest score wala point chuna jaata hai" deterministic hain (L27).

📊 Visual intuition

📖 Formal version (Formula Protocol) — naye L29–L32

L29 — score rule paper-dependent. Standard K-means++: $s_j(x)=D_j(x)^2$, $\Pr(\mu_j=x)=\dfrac{s_j(x)}{\sum_{x'}s_j(x')}$. Par sawaal ka apna rule ho sakta hai ($s=D$, ya Manhattan). Is bank mein: 2024 Jul (P4 of B20) squared ✓ key; 2024 Oct (C6) "squared distance" likha; 2025 Feb (P3 yahan) key plain $D$ se; 2025 Oct (P5) "twice" statement squared se; 2026 Mar (P6) key squared se. Niyam: pehle sawaal ka phrase padho; phir key se check karo; tabhi $D$ ya $D^2$ lagao.

Symbols: $D_j(x)=\min_{l\lt j}\|x-\mu_l\|$ (nearest of all chune hue); $s_j$ score; $\mu_j$ $j$-wa centre. Meaning in words: naya centre usi point ke chune jaane ki sambhavna uski "nearest centre se doori" ke hisaab se badhti hai.

L30 — probability ratio = score ratio. Do points $x,y$ ke liye $\dfrac{\Pr(x)}{\Pr(y)}=\dfrac{s(x)}{s(y)}$ — $\sum s$ cancel. $D^2$ rule: ratio $=\left(\dfrac{D_x}{D_y}\right)^2$; $D$ rule: ratio $=\dfrac{D_x}{D_y}$. Notice that… "A ki probability B se do guna" jaise statement ke liye normalise karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas ratio dekho (P5 stmt 2).

L31 — ties. Agar $\|x-\mu_a\|=\|x-\mu_b\|$ ho to assign step ko tie-break rule chahiye (jaise "chhote index wala centre"). Alag tie-breaks alag partition de sakte hain, aur ek hi data par alag final centres aur kabhi alag final $F$ (P5: $F=16/3$ vs $4$). Yeh local-optimum ka ek aur roop hai (L22).

L32 — iteration-count convention. "Kitni iterations mein converge?" ke do padhne ke tareeke: A — aakhri iteration jisme kuch badla (partition ya centres); B — A $+1$ (woh confirming pass jisme kuch nahi badla). Is bank mein 2025 Jul Q3 (B19 P3) aur 2025 Oct Q18 (B19 P6) ki keys A ke saath chalti hain; 2023 Jul Q3 (B18 P1) B ke saath. Options ya key se padho.

Important insight: K-means++ ka randomness sirf starting centres mein hai; uske baad Lloyd deterministic hai (tie-break fix ho to). This is why K-means++ ke baad bhi Lloyd chalana padta hai aur global optimum ki guarantee nahi milti.

⚙️ How it works — numbered mechanism (variations ke saath)

  1. Rule padho: sawaal score rule ($D$/$D^2$, Euclid/Manhattan) ya "highest score chuna jaata hai" likhta hai? Na likha ho to key/standard $D^2$ (L29).
  2. Chune hue centres likho (pehle se diye hue sab, sirf $\mu_1$ nahi).
  3. Har point ka $D$ = un sab centres mein se nearest (L25). Chune hue points $\Rightarrow D=0$, score $0$ (L26).
  4. Score $s=D^2$ (ya rule ke hisaab se).
  5. Sawaal ki cheez: sirf ranking? ⇒ step 4 par ruko (L24). Number? ⇒ $\sum s$ nikalo, $s/\sum s$ (L23). Ratio? ⇒ L30.
  6. Agla centre judne par step 3 se dobara (ranking flip ho sakti hai, WE5).
  7. Lloyd tak aao: ties dikhein to tie-break kya hai, aur iteration count ki convention kya hai (L31, L32).

🔬 Deeper insight (D3)

Kyun squared? aur $\sum s=F$ ka istemaal

(a) $\sum s=F$ (L28). Squared rule mein agla centre chunne se pehle $\sum_x D(x)^2$ = abhi ke chune hue centres ke saath nearest-centre assignment ka $F$. P2 mein centres $(2,1),(2,4)$ ke saath $\sum=4+2+2+1+2=11=F$ (code ✓).

(b) Kyun $D^2$? $F$ khud squared distances ka jod hai; $D^2$ se sampling "mushkil se cover hone wale" regions ko zyada tickets deti hai. Ek (syllabus ke bahar) guarantee bhi hai ki K-means++ ka expected $F$ optimum ke $O(\log K)$ factor ke andar rehta hai [ADDED] — exam ke liye zaroori nahi, bas yaad rakho ki yeh "global optimum" nahi hai.

(c) Duplicate points. Agar do points ke coordinates same hon aur ek centre ban jaaye, to doosre ka $D=0$ ⇒ score $0$ — woh bhi nahi chuna jaayega.

(d) Convention table. $F$ sequence $800,500,400,400,400$ (B19 P3): A ⇒ iteration 3; B ⇒ iteration 4. P5 stmt 3: A ⇒ 1; B ⇒ 2 (neeche).

📌 Memory card — 4.5 K-means++ (A + B)

Core model: K-means++ = starting centres ka randomized chunav: $\mu_1$ koi data point; har agla centre uske score ($D^2$, ya sawaal ka rule) ke hisse ke hisaab se; phir wahi Lloyd. Randomness sirf start mein.

Backbone chain: rule padho → chune hue centres → har point ka $D$ (nearest of all) → score → ranking ya $s/\sum s$ → chuna hua $=0$ → dohrao → Lloyd (ties + iteration convention).

Formulas (with conditions):

  • $D_j(x)=\min_{l\lt j}\|x-\mu_l\|$; $\Pr(\mu_j=x)=\dfrac{s_j(x)}{\sum_{x'}s_j(x')}$, standard $s=D^2$
  • Ratio: $\dfrac{\Pr(x)}{\Pr(y)}=\dfrac{s(x)}{s(y)}$ — $D^2$ mein $(D_x/D_y)^2$
  • $\sum s=F$ (squared rule, nearest assignment) · naya centre judne par score ghat-te hain, ranking flip ho sakti hai
  • "Highest score chuna jaata hai" ⇒ deterministic (L27)
  • Ties ⇒ tie-break final centres badal sakta hai ($F$ kabhi, hamesha nahi)

Top 3 traps: (1) 3rd centre mein sirf $\mu_1$ se doori (nearest-of-all bhoolna; B20 P2/P4, B21 P2/P6). (2) Score rule na padhna — $D$ vs $D^2$ (B21 P3 ki key $D$, P6 ki key $D^2$) aur "highest probability" = "chuna jayega" samajhna. (3) Ties aur iteration-count convention ko ignore karna (P5), ya mismatch par key ko bina data check ke maan lena ([FLAG]).

Trigger bank: "highest/lowest probability" ⇒ ranking · "probability of $x$" ⇒ $s/\sum s$ · "3rd mean / after second" ⇒ min over both · "highest score chosen" ⇒ argmax · "twice / equal probability" ⇒ score ratio · "closest mean (Euclid)" ⇒ $d^2$ / bisector · "converge in $m$ iterations" ⇒ convention + ties · "enhances initialization" ⇒ spread, no guarantees.
