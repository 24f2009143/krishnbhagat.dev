Top 5 traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. Label / ones–zeros ulta — $p=P(A)$ ya $P(1)$? 5.1 P1–P4 isi par tike hain (agar $p=P(x=0)$ hota to $30$ aate, $70$ nahi).
  2. Pooling ki jagah averaging, aur geometric mein success toss ya $t-1$ failures galat ginna (5.1 P5: $\tfrac19=0.111$).
  3. Exponential: mean ko rate maanna; log-likelihood options mein $\prod$ / bina-$\ln$; frequency table mein $n=\sum c_j$; Pareto mein $\sum x_i$ ya $\log_{10}$.
  4. Beta update — ones $\to\alpha$, zeros $\to\beta$; poora $n$ ($\beta+n$) mat jodo (5.3 P2 ka galat $0.25$ isi se aata hai).
  5. Mean vs mode vs MLE vs prior mean — 5.3 P5 mein chaaron paas hain: $0.600$ (mean), $0.611$ (mode), $0.615$ (MLE), $0.571$ (prior mean). Shabd padho.

  2. RT2 · SA · 3 marks. Aap ek coin $3$ baar toss karte hain: koi head nahi. Phir dost usi coin ko head aane tak toss karta hai aur use $4$ tosses lagte hain (T, T, T, H). $p=P(\text{head})$ ka MLE (3 decimals)?

  4. RT4 · SA · 3 marks. Poisson model. $30$ points: value $0$ aaya $6$ baar, $1$ aaya $9$ baar, $2$ aaya $9$ baar, $4$ aaya $6$ baar. $\hat\lambda_{MLE}$ (2 decimals)?

RT1: 66. Type B $=n(1-\hat p)=120\times0.55=66$. Trap: $120\times0.45=54$ (A ki ginti).

RT2: 0.143. Pehle experiment: $3$ tails. Dost: $(1-p)^3p$. Total $L=(1-p)^6p$ ⇒ heads $1$, tosses $7$ ⇒ $\hat p=\tfrac17=0.143$. Trap: dono estimates ka average ($0$ aur $\tfrac14$ ⇒ $0.125$), ya dost ke $4$ tosses mein se success toss na ginna. Code: numerical maximiser $0.142857$ ✓.

RT3: 0.333. $\hat\lambda=n/\sum x=4/12=0.333$ ($=1/\bar x$, $\bar x=3$). Trap: $\bar x=3$ ko rate likhna.

RT4: 1.70. $\sum x=0\cdot6+1\cdot9+2\cdot9+4\cdot6=51$, $n=\sum c=30$ ⇒ $\tfrac{51}{30}=1.70$. Trap: $\tfrac{51}4=12.75$ (distinct values se divide).

RT5: 1.681. $\sum\ln x=0.4055+0.6931+0.1823+1.0986=2.3795$ ⇒ $\hat\theta=\tfrac4{2.3795}=1.681$ ($>1$ ✓). Code: numerical maximiser $1.680993$ ✓. Trap: $\log_{10}$ ya $\sum x$.

RT6: $\hat\theta=2$. $L(2)=\prod2x_i=8\times0.9\times0.8\times0.3=1.728\gt1=L(1)$; equivalently $\sum\ln(2x_i)=0.547\gt0$. Trap: label ulat dena ($\theta=1\leftrightarrow f=2x$).

RT7: 0.340. Posterior Beta$(2+14,\ 5+26)=$ Beta(16,31); mean $\tfrac{16}{47}=0.340$. Bound check: prior mean $0.286$ aur MLE $0.35$ ke beech ✓. Trap: $\beta+n$ ⇒ $\tfrac{16}{61}=0.262$; mode $\tfrac{15}{45}=0.333$; MLE $0.35$.

RT8: (a), (c). (a) Beta$(1+5,\ 1+15)=$ Beta(6,16) ✓. (b) ✗ posterior mean $\tfrac6{22}=0.273$; $0.25$ mode/MLE hai. (c) ✓ mode $\tfrac{5}{20}=0.25$ (Beta(1,1) par MAP $=$ MLE). (d) ✗ Beta(1,1) ek "1 one + 1 zero" pseudo-count jaisa hai. (e) ✗ $L$ ka $p$ par area $1$ nahi hota (data par probability hai, $p$ par nahi).
