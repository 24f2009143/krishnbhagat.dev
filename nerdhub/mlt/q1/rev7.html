🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

T7 kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]: K-means har point ko ek hi cluster deta hai aur sirf mean seekhta hai. GMM kehta hai: har point kisi chhupe component $z$ se aaya hai; hum $z$ nahi dekhte, isliye $\pi,\mu,\sigma^2$ ka MLE seedha nahi milta ($\ln$ ke andar sum). EM is mushkil ko do aasan kadam mein todta hai: pehle andaza lagao "kaun kahan se aaya" ($\gamma$), phir un weights se parameters ka MLE nikalo.

Top 5 traps (subtopic pages se):

  1. Prior $\pi$ chhodna ya $\gamma$ ko $\pi$/$f$ samajhna; galat table row; $\sigma$ vs $\sigma^2$; $p(x)$ do numbers se nikalne ki koshish (7.3 P9).
  2. $\ln$ ko sum ke andar todna, $\prod_k$ ya $\sum_i\sum_k$; factor $3$ ($f_2=3e^{-3x}$) ya $\pi$ chhodna (7.2).
  3. M-step mein $n$ ya $K$ se divide ($N_k$ chahiye); $\sigma^2$ mein purana $\mu$; $\pi_k$ ko point-level $\lambda$ se milana (7.4 P2: $0.8$, $\pi_2=0.5$ nahi).
  4. $\sum\gamma$ ki jagah mean; MAP rule ulta ($\gamma\le0.5\Rightarrow$ comp 2); hard cut-off $\gamma>0.5$ se count karna (soft weight chahiye).
  5. "Converges" = "global" (EM aur Lloyd dono local); "init does not affect"; EM ke $\ell\uparrow$ ko global max samajhna.
