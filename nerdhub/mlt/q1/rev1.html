🖼️ 1. Topic big picture

T1 kyun exist karta hai [ADDED]: PCA ka poora idea ek line par projection hai: "kaunsi line par data ko girane se sabse kam information jaati hai?" Is sawaal ke do hisse hain — (1.1) point ka line par projection kaise nikalte hain, aur (1.2) jo bacha (residual) uski size kitni hai. T2 mein yahi do cheezein "PC coordinate" aur "reconstruction error" ban jaati hain.

Topic ki "exam story" (1–2 line) [ADDED]: "Direction $w$ diya hai — $w$ ko normalise karo, dot lo, aur chain mein jo maanga ho (length / vector / error) wahan ruko." Is topic mein marks $\|w\|$ bhoolne aur norm vs squared mein jaate hain, concept mein nahi.

🔑 3. Exam pattern digest

Bank mein T1 [SRC]: ek hi comprehension — 2024 October 27, Q11 (640653995437, context) ke do child: Q12 (…438, 3 marks, projection length) aur Q13 (…439, 4 marks, reconstruction-error norm). Dono ek hi $x=(3,4)$, $w=(1,1)$ par. Jawab: $4.95$ aur $0.71$.

Recurring question typeTrigger phrase → first move
Length of projection"length of the projection", "line defined by $w$" → pehle $\|w\|$ nikalo, $1$ nahi hai to divide
Scalar projection / coordinate"scalar projection onto first PC" (unit diya) → seedha $x^Tw$, phir bhi $\|w\|=1$ verify
Projection vector / reconstructed point"projection vector", "reconstructed point" → pehle scalar, phir unit vector se guna
Magnitude of reconstruction error"norm of reconstruction error" → $s$ (exact) → $\sqrt{\|x\|^2-s^2}$

Top 5 traps (B3 se):

  1. $w$ normalise na karna ($7$ instead of $4.95$; $7>\|x\|$ impossible).
  2. $\|w\|^2$ se divide karke "length" bolna (woh coefficient $t^*=3.5$ hai).
  3. Norm vs squared mix ($0.71$ vs $0.5$).
  4. Lengths subtract karna ($5-4.95=0.05\neq0.71$) — Pythagoras use karo.
  5. Intermediate rounding ($4.95^2=24.5025$ vs exact $24.5$): bade numbers mein answer bigad jaata hai; exact rakho, end mein round karo. Vector answer dena jab number maanga ho (ya ulta) — format check.

Method selector [ADDED]: sirf number → unit + dot · aage residual bhi chahiye → vector $p$ pehle · sirf error norm → Pythagoras · 2-D distance → normal vector · residual vector options → $r^Tw=0$.

RT1: 7.54. $x^Tw=10-108=-98$; $\|w\|=13$; $s=-98/13=-7.538$; length $=|s|=7.54$. (Trap: $-7.54$, ya $98/169=0.58$.)

RT2: 5.31. $\|x\|^2=4+81=85$; $s^2=(98/13)^2=56.83$; $\|r\|^2=28.17$; $\|r\|=5.308$. Check: $p=\tfrac{-98}{169}(5,12)$, $r=x-p$ ka norm $5.3077$ ✓.

RT3: (b). (a) $=|t^*|$ — coefficient hai, length nahi ($w$ ki length ke bina). (c) aur (d) mein $\|w\|$ galat jagah / bilkul nahi — $w$ ko double karo to length double ho jaati, jabki projection ki length $w$ ke scale par depend nahi karti.

RT4: (a), (b), (d). (a) $x^T(-u)(-u)=(x^Tu)u$, $p$ same ⇒ $r$ same. (b) Pythagoras. (c) ✗ — $|x^Tu|$ nahi, uska square. (d) $x=tu\Rightarrow x^Tu=t$, $p=tu=x$, $r=0$. (e) ✗ — $\|p\|^2+\|r\|^2=\|x\|^2$, lengths ka jod nahi (triangle inequality: $\|p\|+\|r\|\ge\|x\|$).
