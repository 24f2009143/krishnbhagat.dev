Privacy

The study sites work fully without signing in. Progress then stays in your own browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

If you choose Sign in to sync, you sign in with a Google account ending in @ds.study.iitm.ac.in. Then I store, in a Google Firebase database:

  • your email address,
  • your study progress: which sections you read, your quiz answers and results, flashcard and mistake lists, and your settings,
  • when it was last updated.

Only you and I (Krishn Bhagat, the site owner) can read it. I use it to sync your devices and to see which topics students find hard, so I can improve the sites. I don't sell it or share it, and I don't use ad trackers.

To delete your cloud copy, use Reset all progress in the site's settings while signed in, or email me and I'll delete it.
